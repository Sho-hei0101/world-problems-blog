## Problem overview

As fixed mortgage rates continue to rise, many borrowers are reevaluating their options. The traditional fixed-rate mortgage has long been a staple for homebuyers seeking stability in their monthly payments. However, with the increasing cost of borrowing, adjustable-rate mortgages (ARMs) are becoming more attractive. This shift raises questions about the future of the mortgage market and how it affects both borrowers and lenders.

## FAQ

**What is an adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM)?**

An ARM is a type of mortgage where the interest rate can change at specified times, usually resulting in lower initial payments.

**Why are borrowers choosing ARMs over fixed-rate mortgages?**

Borrowers are opting for ARMs due to rising fixed rates, which make ARMs more appealing with their lower initial rates.

**What are the risks associated with ARMs?**

The primary risk is that interest rates may increase, leading to higher monthly payments after the initial fixed period ends.

**How can I decide if an ARM is right for me?**

Consider your financial situation, how long you plan to stay in your home, and your comfort level with potential rate changes.

**What should I look for when comparing mortgage options?**

Examine the interest rate, loan terms, fees, and whether the loan is fixed or adjustable.
