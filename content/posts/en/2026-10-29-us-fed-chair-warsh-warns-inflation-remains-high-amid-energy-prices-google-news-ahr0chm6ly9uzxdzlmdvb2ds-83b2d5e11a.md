## FAQ

**What is inflation?**

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises, leading to a decrease in purchasing power.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Rising energy prices increase the cost of transportation and production, which can lead to higher prices for goods and services.

**What can the Federal Reserve do about inflation?**

The Federal Reserve can adjust interest rates and implement monetary policy measures to control inflation and stabilize the economy.
