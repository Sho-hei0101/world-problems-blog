## Problem overview

In recent years, the world has faced a series of challenges that have contributed to rising inflation and recession risks. Wars and geopolitical tensions have disrupted global supply chains, particularly in the energy sector. As energy prices soar, the cost of living increases, leading to a ripple effect across economies. Simultaneously, central banks are raising interest rates in an attempt to combat inflation, which can further exacerbate economic instability. The chemicals industry, being closely tied to energy prices, is particularly sensitive to these changes, affecting everything from production costs to consumer prices.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of these issues are not confined to specific regions; they are felt worldwide. Rising inflation can erode purchasing power, leading to decreased consumer spending. As interest rates rise, borrowing costs increase, impacting both businesses and consumers. This scenario can stifle economic growth and lead to recessions, affecting employment rates and overall economic health. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for policymakers, businesses, and consumers alike as they navigate an increasingly complex economic landscape.
