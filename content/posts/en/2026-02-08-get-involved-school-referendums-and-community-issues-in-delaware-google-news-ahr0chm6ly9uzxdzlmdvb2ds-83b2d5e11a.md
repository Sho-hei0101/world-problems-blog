## FAQ

**What is a school referendum?**

A school referendum is a voting process where community members decide on funding measures for local schools, often to improve facilities or programs.

**How can I get involved in hospital oversight?**

You can engage with local health boards, attend public meetings, and advocate for transparency and accountability in healthcare services.

**What can I do about rising energy prices?**

Stay informed about local energy policies, participate in community discussions, and explore options for energy efficiency and renewable sources.

**Why is community involvement important?**

Community involvement ensures that local governance reflects the needs of residents, leading to better decision-making and improved quality of life.

**Where can I find more information on these issues?**

Local news outlets, community forums, and government websites are excellent resources for staying informed about school referendums, hospital oversight, and energy prices.
