## FAQ

**What causes inflation to rise?**

Inflation can rise due to increased demand for goods and services, higher production costs, and supply chain disruptions.

**How does inflation affect everyday consumers?**

Inflation leads to higher prices for goods and services, reducing purchasing power and potentially leading to financial strain.

**What can governments do to control inflation?**

Governments can implement monetary policies, such as raising interest rates, and fiscal policies to manage demand and supply.

**Is inflation always bad for the economy?**

Moderate inflation can be a sign of a growing economy, but high inflation can lead to economic instability and decreased confidence.

**How can individuals protect themselves from inflation?**

Individuals can invest in assets that typically appreciate during inflation, such as real estate or commodities, and budget wisely.
