## FAQ

**What are the main causes of the energy crisis affecting food prices?**

The energy crisis is driven by geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, and rising demand, which collectively increase energy prices and, subsequently, food production costs.

**How can consumers prepare for rising food prices?**
