## Problem overview

The mortgage industry is increasingly reliant on technology to streamline processes. However, the fragmentation of these technologies has led to inefficiencies, resulting in what is termed the 'interoperability tax.' This tax refers to the hidden costs incurred when different technological systems fail to communicate effectively with one another. As a result, lenders, brokers, and other stakeholders face delays, increased operational costs, and a negative impact on customer service. Understanding the intricacies of this issue is crucial for navigating the modern mortgage landscape.
