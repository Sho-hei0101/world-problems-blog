## FAQ

**What factors contribute to rising energy prices?**

Geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, and increased demand can all lead to higher energy prices.

**How does rising inflation affect consumers?**

Inflation erodes purchasing power, meaning consumers can buy less with the same amount of money.

**What can governments do to stabilize energy prices?**

Governments can implement policies to promote energy efficiency, diversify energy sources, and provide subsidies to vulnerable populations.

**Are there ways to reduce personal energy costs?**
