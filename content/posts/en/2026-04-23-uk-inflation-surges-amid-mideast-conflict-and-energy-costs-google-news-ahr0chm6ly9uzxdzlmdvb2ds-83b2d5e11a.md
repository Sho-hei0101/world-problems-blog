## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation is caused by various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, rising production costs, and external shocks like geopolitical conflicts.

**How does inflation affect consumers?**

Inflation erodes purchasing power, making goods and services more expensive, which can strain household budgets and savings.

**What can individuals do to manage inflation's effects?**

Individuals can budget better, prioritize essential spending, seek alternative energy sources, and consider investments that can hedge against inflation.

**Is inflation temporary or long-lasting?**

It can be both. Some inflation can be temporary due to specific events, while other factors may lead to longer-term inflationary trends.

**How does the government respond to rising inflation?**

Governments may adjust interest rates, implement fiscal policies, or provide support to affected sectors to manage inflation.
