## FAQ

**What are mortgage rates?**

Mortgage rates are the interest rates charged on a mortgage loan, which can vary based on economic factors and government policies.

**How can the government lower mortgage rates?**

Governments can lower mortgage rates through monetary policy adjustments, subsidies for homebuyers, and support for affordable housing initiatives.

**Why are low mortgage rates important?**

Low mortgage rates make homeownership more affordable, stimulate the housing market, and can boost economic growth.

**What is the role of the Federal Reserve?**

The Federal Reserve influences mortgage rates by adjusting the federal funds rate, which affects overall interest rates in the economy.

**Can I influence mortgage rates as an individual?**

While individual actions might not directly change mortgage rates, advocating for policy changes and participating in community discussions can contribute to broader efforts.
