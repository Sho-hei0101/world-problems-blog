## FAQ

**What are relief checks?**

Relief checks are direct payments made by the government to help individuals and families cope with rising costs.

**How do tax cuts help with the cost of living?**

Tax cuts can increase disposable income for individuals, allowing them to spend more on essentials and alleviate financial stress.

**What role do housing policies play in addressing living costs?**

Housing policies can help regulate rental prices, promote affordable housing, and improve overall living conditions, thereby reducing financial burdens.
