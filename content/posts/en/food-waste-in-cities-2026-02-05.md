# Understanding Food Waste in Urban America

Food waste is a pressing issue in cities across the United States, with millions of tons discarded every year. This not only impacts the environment but also contributes to hunger in our communities. 

## The Scale of the Problem
In urban areas, approximately 30-40% of the food supply goes to waste. Cities like Los Angeles and New York are taking steps to combat this issue by implementing food recovery programs and encouraging local businesses to donate excess food.

## Innovative Solutions
- **Community Fridges**: Initiatives in cities like Philadelphia and Chicago provide communal refrigerators stocked with food donations, available to anyone in need.
- **Educational Campaigns**: Programs aimed at teaching residents about meal planning and proper food storage can significantly reduce household waste.

## Conclusion
By fostering community involvement and supporting local initiatives, we can create a sustainable future and minimize food waste in our cities.
