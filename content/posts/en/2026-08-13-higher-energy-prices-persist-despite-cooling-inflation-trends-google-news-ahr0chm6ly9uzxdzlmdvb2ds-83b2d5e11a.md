## Practical actions you can take

To navigate the challenges posed by high energy prices, consumers and businesses can adopt several practical strategies. Here are some actionable steps that can help mitigate the financial impact of soaring energy costs.

- Monitor energy usage and identify areas to reduce consumption.
- Consider upgrading to energy-efficient appliances.
- Explore alternative energy options, such as solar power.
- Stay informed about government assistance programs.
- Plan budgets to accommodate higher energy costs.
- Advocate for energy policy changes that promote sustainability.
- Engage in community programs focused on energy conservation.
- Review and adjust contracts for energy supply where possible.
