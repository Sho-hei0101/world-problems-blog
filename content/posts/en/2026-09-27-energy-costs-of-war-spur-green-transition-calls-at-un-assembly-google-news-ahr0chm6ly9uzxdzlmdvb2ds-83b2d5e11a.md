## Problem overview

The escalating energy costs associated with military operations, particularly in conflict zones, are prompting global discussions on the urgent need for a green energy transition. As armies consume vast amounts of fossil fuels, the environmental impact is significant, leading to calls for sustainable alternatives. The UN General Assembly serves as a platform for these discussions, emphasizing the need to address the energy demands of war in the context of climate change and sustainability.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of energy consumption during warfare extend beyond immediate military needs; they contribute to global warming, resource depletion, and environmental degradation. As nations face the dual challenge of managing military expenditures and addressing climate change, the call for a green transition becomes increasingly relevant. This is not just a national concern but a global imperative, as the effects of climate change are felt worldwide, exacerbating existing conflicts and creating new ones.
