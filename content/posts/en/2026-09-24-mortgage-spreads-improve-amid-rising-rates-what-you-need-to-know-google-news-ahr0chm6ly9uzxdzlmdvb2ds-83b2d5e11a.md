## FAQ

**What are mortgage spreads?**

Mortgage spreads refer to the difference between mortgage rates and the yields on government bonds, such as the 10-year Treasury note.

**How does the 10-year yield affect mortgage rates?**

Typically, when the 10-year Treasury yield rises, mortgage rates tend to increase as well, affecting borrowing costs for homebuyers.

**Should I buy a home now or wait for rates to stabilize?**

Deciding whether to buy now or wait depends on your financial situation and market conditions. Consulting with a financial advisor can help you make an informed choice.
