## Problem overview

The Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) has forecasted a significant decrease in mortgage volume, projecting it to dip below $2 trillion by 2027. This forecast is primarily driven by persistently high interest rates, averaging around 7%. Such a decline poses challenges for homebuyers, lenders, and the overall housing market. The increased cost of borrowing can deter potential buyers, leading to reduced demand and, consequently, lower home prices. This situation creates a ripple effect that can impact various sectors of the economy, including construction, home improvement, and real estate services.
