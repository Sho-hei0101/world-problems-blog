## FAQ

**What causes the rise in energy bills?**

The rise in energy bills is often driven by increased demand, limited supply, and geopolitical factors affecting energy markets.

**How can I reduce my energy costs?**

Consider energy-saving measures like using energy-efficient appliances, adjusting heating settings, and reducing unnecessary energy use.

**What support is available for those struggling with cost of living increases?**

Various government programs and charities offer assistance, including financial aid, food banks, and energy grants.

**How does inflation affect my purchasing power?**

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money, meaning you can buy less with the same amount of income as prices rise.

**What long-term strategies can I implement?**

Consider budgeting, exploring additional income sources, and investing in skills or education to enhance earning potential.
