## Problem overview

As we approach 2027, hospitals are bracing for significant financial challenges due to the dual impact of artificial intelligence (AI) integration and escalating energy prices. These factors are expected to strain non-labor spending, which includes essential operational costs such as supplies, utilities, and maintenance. The rise of AI in healthcare is intended to improve efficiency and patient outcomes; however, the initial investment and ongoing costs associated with these technologies can divert funds from other critical areas. Additionally, the volatility of energy prices adds another layer of complexity, forcing hospitals to reassess their budgets and spending strategies.
