## FAQ

**What causes inflation in Switzerland?**

Inflation in Switzerland is primarily driven by rising energy prices, but can also be influenced by supply chain issues and increased consumer demand.

**How does inflation affect consumers?**

Inflation decreases purchasing power, meaning consumers can buy less with the same amount of money, leading to a higher cost of living.

**What measures can the Swiss government take to control inflation?**

The Swiss government can implement monetary policy adjustments, such as altering interest rates, and fiscal measures to stabilize the economy.

**Is inflation expected to continue rising?**
