## Practical actions you can take

To effectively prepare for wildfire smoke, it’s important to take proactive measures. Here are some actionable steps you can implement to ensure your safety and that of your loved ones during smoke events.

- Monitor local air quality regularly.
- Create an emergency plan for smoke events.
- Ensure your home has proper air filtration.
- Stock up on N95 masks for respiratory protection.
- Prepare an emergency kit with essentials like water, food, and medications.
- Stay informed about local wildfire activity and advisories.
- Limit outdoor activities during high smoke days.
- Educate your family about the health risks of smoke exposure.

## FAQ

**What are the health risks associated with wildfire smoke?**

Wildfire smoke can cause respiratory issues, irritation of eyes and throat, and can exacerbate existing health conditions like asthma and heart disease.

**How can I monitor air quality during wildfire season?**

You can use local air quality websites, mobile apps, or air quality index (AQI) indicators to stay informed about current conditions.

**What steps should I take if smoke levels are high?**

Stay indoors, use air purifiers, keep windows and doors closed, and limit outdoor activities.

**Are there resources available for those affected by wildfire smoke?**

Yes, local health departments often provide resources and information on smoke safety, and emergency services can offer assistance.

**How can I prepare my home for wildfire smoke?**

Ensure your home has good air filtration systems, stock up on N95 masks, and create a smoke emergency kit with essentials.
