## Why this matters globally

Understanding energy prices is crucial on a global scale for several reasons. Energy is foundational to economic stability, and significant price changes can lead to inflation, altered consumer behavior, and shifts in energy policy. Additionally, regions heavily dependent on energy imports are particularly vulnerable to price hikes, which can exacerbate existing economic disparities. The normalization of energy prices post-conflict is vital for rebuilding economies and ensuring a stable energy future.
