## Practical actions you can take

While the situation may seem dire, there are practical steps individuals and families can take to navigate the rising costs of living. By being proactive and informed, you can better manage your finances and mitigate the impact of soaring prices.

- Consider energy-efficient appliances to reduce energy bills.
