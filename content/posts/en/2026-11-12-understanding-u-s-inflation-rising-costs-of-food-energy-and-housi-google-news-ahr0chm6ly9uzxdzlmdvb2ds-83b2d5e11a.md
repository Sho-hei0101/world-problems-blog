## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation can be caused by various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, rising production costs, and monetary policies.

**How does inflation affect my savings?**

Inflation erodes the purchasing power of money, meaning that over time, your savings may buy less if not invested wisely.

**What can I do to protect myself from inflation?**

Consider diversifying investments, budgeting carefully, and looking for ways to increase income to offset rising costs.

**Is inflation expected to continue rising?**

While predictions vary, many economists suggest that inflation may remain elevated due to ongoing supply chain issues and increased consumer demand.

**How often do inflation rates change?**

Inflation rates are typically reported monthly by the Bureau of Labor Statistics in the U.S., reflecting changes in consumer prices.
