## FAQ

**What are proprietary loans?**

Proprietary loans are private loans offered by lenders that are not federally insured, allowing for higher loan amounts and more flexible terms.

**Who qualifies for a proprietary loan?**

Typically, homeowners aged 62 and older who have significant home equity can qualify for proprietary loans.

**What are the risks associated with proprietary loans?**

Risks include high fees, the potential for foreclosure if property taxes or insurance are not paid, and the impact on heirs' inheritance.

**How do proprietary loans differ from traditional reverse mortgages?**

Proprietary loans generally offer larger loan amounts and more flexible eligibility criteria compared to traditional reverse mortgages.

**Can I use a proprietary loan for any purpose?**

Yes, homeowners can use funds from a proprietary loan for various purposes, including home renovations, medical expenses, or daily living costs.
