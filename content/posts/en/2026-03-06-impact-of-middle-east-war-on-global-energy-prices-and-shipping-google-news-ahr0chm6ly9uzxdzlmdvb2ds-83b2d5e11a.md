## FAQ

**How do energy prices affect shipping costs?**

Higher energy prices increase fuel costs for shipping, leading to increased overall shipping expenses.

**What can businesses do to prepare for energy price fluctuations?**

Businesses can diversify their supply chains, invest in energy-efficient technologies, and monitor geopolitical developments closely.

**Why is the Middle East conflict significant for global trade?**

The Middle East is a key supplier of oil and gas; conflicts can disrupt supply and increase prices globally.

**What are the economic consequences of shipping disruptions?**
