## Problem overview

In Delaware, there exists a significant disparity between rental and residential property tax rates, which has sparked a debate about fairness and economic impact. The current structure places a heavier tax burden on rental properties compared to owner-occupied homes. This discrepancy can lead to increased rental prices, thereby affecting affordability for tenants. Meyer’s proposed bill aims to address this issue by aligning the tax rates, creating a more equitable tax environment for all property types.
