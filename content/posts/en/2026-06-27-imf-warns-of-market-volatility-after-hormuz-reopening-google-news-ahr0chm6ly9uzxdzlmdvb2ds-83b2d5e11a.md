## FAQ

**What is the Strait of Hormuz and why is it important?**

The Strait of Hormuz is a narrow waterway that connects the Persian Gulf to the Arabian Sea, and it's a critical transit route for a significant portion of the world's oil supply.

**What does the IMF mean by market volatility?**

Market volatility refers to the fluctuations in prices and trading volumes in financial markets, often influenced by various economic and geopolitical factors.

**How can individuals prepare for economic volatility?**

Individuals can prepare by diversifying investments, staying informed about market trends, and having a financial plan that accounts for potential economic shifts.

**What impact does energy price fluctuation have on consumers?**

Fluctuating energy prices can lead to increased costs for utilities, transportation, and goods, ultimately affecting consumer purchasing power and inflation rates.

**What strategies can businesses adopt to mitigate risks?**

Businesses can adopt strategies such as hedging against price fluctuations, diversifying supply chains, and adjusting pricing strategies to maintain profitability.
