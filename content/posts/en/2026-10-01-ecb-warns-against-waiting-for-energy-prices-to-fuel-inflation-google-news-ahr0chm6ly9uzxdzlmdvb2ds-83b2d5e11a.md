A top policymaker has emphasized that the ECB cannot afford to wait for these high energy prices to lead to a more pervasive inflationary environment. This situation raises concerns about the potential for prolonged economic instability if appropriate measures are not taken promptly.

## FAQ

**What is the ECB's role in managing inflation?**

The ECB is responsible for monetary policy in the Eurozone and aims to maintain price stability by controlling inflation.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Rising energy prices increase production costs, which can lead to higher prices for goods and services, contributing to overall inflation.
