## Problem overview

As retirement approaches, many individuals face financial challenges. Traditional savings may fall short, prompting a need for alternative income sources. Reverse mortgages, which allow homeowners aged 62 and older to convert part of their home equity into cash, have gained attention as a viable option. ## FAQ

**What is a reverse mortgage?**

A reverse mortgage is a loan available to homeowners aged 62 and older that allows them to convert part of their home equity into cash without having to sell their home.

**What are the benefits of a reverse mortgage?**

Benefits include increased cash flow during retirement, no monthly mortgage payments, and the ability to stay in your home while accessing funds.

**Are there risks associated with reverse mortgages?**

Yes, risks include potentially owing more than the home's value, affecting inheritance, and fees that can reduce equity.

**How can I determine if a reverse mortgage is right for me?**

Consult a financial advisor and consider your financial situation, retirement goals, and the long-term implications of taking out a reverse mortgage.

**How do policy changes impact reverse mortgages?**

Changes in government regulations can affect eligibility, fees, and the availability of reverse mortgage products, influencing how they fit into retirement planning.
