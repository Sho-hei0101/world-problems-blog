## FAQ

**What factors are driving mortgage rates up?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by various factors, including inflation, Federal Reserve policies, and overall economic conditions.

**How do rising mortgage rates affect home prices?**

Generally, rising mortgage rates can lead to decreased demand for homes, which may put downward pressure on home prices.

**Is it a good time to buy a home despite high mortgage rates?**

It depends on individual circumstances. Buyers should consider their financial situation and long-term plans before making a decision.

**What should sellers do in a high-rate environment?**

Sellers may need to adjust their pricing strategies and be more flexible with negotiations to attract buyers.

**Will mortgage rates continue to rise?**

While it's difficult to predict, many analysts believe rates may stabilize or fluctuate based on economic indicators and Federal Reserve actions.
