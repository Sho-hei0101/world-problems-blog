## FAQ

**Why are mortgage rates rising?**

Mortgage rates are rising primarily due to increasing inflation, which leads to higher borrowing costs as lenders adjust to the economic environment.

**How does this affect homebuyers?**

Higher mortgage rates can reduce purchasing power, making homes less affordable and potentially leading to fewer buyers in the market.

**What can homebuyers do to cope with rising rates?**

Homebuyers can consider locking in rates when they find favorable terms, exploring different loan options, or adjusting their budget to accommodate higher costs.
