## Problem overview

The ongoing climate crisis is leading to significant uninsured losses, which are increasingly affecting the cost of living for individuals and communities worldwide. As extreme weather events become more frequent and severe, the economic burden on those least able to afford it grows heavier. This situation is exacerbated by a lack of adequate insurance coverage for many vulnerable populations, leaving them to bear the brunt of climate impacts without any financial safety net. As a result, the costs associated with these losses are often passed on to consumers, leading to a wider cost of living crisis.

## FAQ

**What are uninsured climate losses?**

Uninsured climate losses refer to financial damages caused by climate-related events that are not covered by insurance policies, leaving individuals and communities to bear the costs.

**How do climate losses affect the cost of living?**

Uninsured climate losses can lead to higher prices for goods and services, as businesses pass on the costs associated with climate impacts to consumers, contributing to a cost of living crisis.

**What actions can individuals take to mitigate these issues?**

Individuals can advocate for better insurance policies, support sustainable practices, and engage in community initiatives focused on climate resilience.
