Additionally, the health impacts associated with extreme heat, including heat strokes and respiratory issues, further burden healthcare systems. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for developing policies that protect vulnerable populations and ensure economic resilience.

## FAQ

**How do heatwaves affect household finances?**

Heatwaves increase energy consumption for cooling and can lead to higher electricity bills, impacting household budgets.

**What health risks are associated with heatwaves?**

Heatwaves can cause heat strokes, dehydration, and exacerbate existing health conditions, leading to increased medical expenses.

**What can local governments do to help residents during heatwaves?**

Local governments can implement cooling centers, provide financial assistance programs, and promote energy efficiency initiatives.

**Are there any long-term solutions to address the impact of heatwaves?**

Investing in renewable energy, improving infrastructure, and enhancing community resilience programs are crucial long-term solutions.

**What should individuals do to prepare for future heatwaves?**

Individuals can improve home insulation, plan for energy needs, and stay informed about local weather forecasts and resources.
