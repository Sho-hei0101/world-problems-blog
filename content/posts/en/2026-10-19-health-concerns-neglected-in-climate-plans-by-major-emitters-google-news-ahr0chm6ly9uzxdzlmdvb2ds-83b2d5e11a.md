## Problem overview

Despite the ongoing climate crisis, many of the world's leading carbon emitters are failing to incorporate health considerations into their climate action plans. This oversight not only undermines public health but also hampers the effectiveness of climate strategies aimed at reducing emissions. As climate change continues to escalate, the consequences for health and well-being could be dire, necessitating a more integrated approach to climate policy that prioritizes both environmental and health outcomes.

## Why this matters globally

The neglect of health in climate plans is a global concern as it threatens the well-being of populations worldwide. Poor air quality, heat-related illnesses, and the spread of infectious diseases are just a few health issues exacerbated by climate change. By failing to address these concerns, policymakers risk implementing climate solutions that do not support the health of communities, leading to increased healthcare costs and diminished quality of life. Global cooperation and commitment to health-inclusive climate policies are essential to ensure a sustainable future.

## FAQ

**Why is health often overlooked in climate plans?**

Health considerations are frequently sidelined in favor of economic and environmental priorities, leading to incomplete climate strategies.

**How does climate change directly affect public health?**

Climate change impacts air quality, water supply, and food security, contributing to respiratory diseases, heat-related illnesses, and more.

**What can individuals do to advocate for health-inclusive climate policies?**
