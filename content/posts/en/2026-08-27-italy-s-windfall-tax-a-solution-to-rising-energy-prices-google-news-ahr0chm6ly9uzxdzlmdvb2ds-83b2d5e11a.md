## FAQ

**What is a windfall tax?**

A windfall tax is a one-time tax levied on profits that exceed a certain threshold, typically targeting industries that experience unexpected financial gains.

**How does the windfall tax affect consumers?**

The revenue generated from the windfall tax can be used to subsidize energy costs for consumers, potentially leading to lower bills and financial relief.

**Are there similar policies in other countries?**

Yes, several countries have implemented or proposed similar taxes to address energy price surges and support their citizens.

**What can individuals do to reduce their energy costs?**

Individuals can implement energy-saving measures, such as using energy-efficient appliances, reducing usage during peak hours, and exploring renewable energy options.

**Will this tax impact energy companies negatively?**

While it may reduce profit margins for some energy companies, it aims to balance the economic burden between corporations and consumers.
