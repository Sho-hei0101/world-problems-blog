## FAQ

**What is core inflation?**

Core inflation is the rate of inflation excluding volatile items like food and energy prices, providing a clearer view of long-term inflation trends.

**How does the Iran war affect global energy prices?**

The Iran war can disrupt oil supply chains and create uncertainty in the market, leading to higher energy prices globally.

**What can consumers do to manage rising prices?**

Consumers can budget more carefully, seek alternatives for high-cost items, and stay informed about market trends to make better purchasing decisions.
