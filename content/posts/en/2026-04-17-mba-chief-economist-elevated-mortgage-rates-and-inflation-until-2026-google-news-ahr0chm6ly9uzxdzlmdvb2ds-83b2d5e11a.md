## FAQ

**What causes mortgage rates to rise?**

Mortgage rates typically rise due to inflation, increased demand for loans, and changes in monetary policy by central banks.

**How does inflation affect homebuyers?**

Inflation can reduce purchasing power, making homes less affordable and increasing monthly mortgage payments.

**What should I do if I need to buy a home during this period?**

Consider locking in a rate early, exploring different financing options, and budgeting for potential increases in costs.

**Are there any benefits to high mortgage rates?**

High mortgage rates can lead to less competition in the housing market, potentially allowing buyers to negotiate better prices.
