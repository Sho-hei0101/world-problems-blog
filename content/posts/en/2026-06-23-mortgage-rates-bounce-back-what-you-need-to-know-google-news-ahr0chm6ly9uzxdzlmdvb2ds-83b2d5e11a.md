## FAQ

**What factors contribute to rising mortgage rates?**

Factors include inflation, changes in the Federal Reserve's interest rates, and overall economic conditions.

**How do rising mortgage rates affect homebuyers?**

Higher mortgage rates lead to increased monthly payments, which can reduce affordability and deter potential buyers.

**What should I do if I want to buy a home during rising rates?**

Consider locking in a rate early, explore different mortgage products, and work with a knowledgeable lender.

**Are there regions more affected by rising mortgage rates?**

Yes, regions with higher housing demand may feel the impact more acutely, while others may not see as significant changes.
