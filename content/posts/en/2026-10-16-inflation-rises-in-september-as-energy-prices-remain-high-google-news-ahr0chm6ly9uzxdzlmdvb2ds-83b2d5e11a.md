## FAQ

**What are the main causes of rising inflation?**

Rising inflation is primarily caused by increased costs of goods and services, particularly in energy and raw materials.

**How does inflation affect consumer spending?**

Inflation can lead to decreased consumer spending as higher prices make goods less affordable.

**What can retailers do to cope with inflation?**

Retailers can optimize their supply chains, adjust pricing strategies, and focus on cost management to cope with inflation.

**Are there any benefits to inflation?**

Moderate inflation can encourage spending and investment, as consumers are motivated to purchase before prices rise further.

**How can consumers protect themselves from inflation?**

Consumers can budget effectively, prioritize essential purchases, and consider fixed-rate loans to mitigate the impact of rising prices.
