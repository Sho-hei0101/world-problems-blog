## Practical actions you can take

Taking proactive measures can significantly reduce the health risks associated with wildfire smoke. Here are some practical actions you can implement to protect yourself and your family during wildfire events:

- Stay informed about local wildfire conditions and air quality.
- Create an emergency plan for your family.
- Purchase air purifiers and keep windows closed during smoke events.
- Use N95 masks if you must go outside.
- Keep a supply of water and non-perishable food in case of evacuation.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activities when air quality is poor.
- Monitor health symptoms and seek medical advice if necessary.
- Educate yourself and your community about wildfire smoke impacts.

## FAQ

**What are the health effects of wildfire smoke?**

Wildfire smoke can cause respiratory issues, eye irritation, and exacerbate chronic conditions like asthma and heart disease.

**How can I tell if the air quality is bad due to smoke?**

Check local air quality indexes (AQI) that provide real-time information on pollution levels, including smoke.

**What should I do if I have to go outside during a smoke event?**

Limit outdoor activities, wear a mask rated for smoke, and stay hydrated. Indoor air quality can be improved with air purifiers.

**Are certain groups more at risk from wildfire smoke?**

Yes, children, the elderly, and people with pre-existing health conditions are particularly vulnerable.

**How can communities prepare for wildfire smoke events?**

Communities can create emergency response plans, provide resources for air quality monitoring, and educate residents on safety measures.
