## Problem overview

Rising energy prices have become a pressing issue across Europe, significantly impacting consumers and businesses alike. Factors contributing to this trend include geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, and increased demand for energy in the wake of economic recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic. As energy costs rise, there is a growing concern about affordability and economic stability, prompting the European Union (EU) to take action to curb these escalating prices with a comprehensive plan aimed at ensuring energy security and sustainability.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of rising energy prices extend beyond individual households and businesses; they affect entire economies, particularly in a globalized market. High energy costs can lead to inflation, reduced consumer spending, and increased operational costs for businesses, potentially stalling economic growth. In the context of climate change and the transition to renewable energy sources, managing energy prices is crucial for fostering a sustainable future. The EU's approach could serve as a model for other regions facing similar challenges, underlining the importance of coordinated policy responses to energy crises.
