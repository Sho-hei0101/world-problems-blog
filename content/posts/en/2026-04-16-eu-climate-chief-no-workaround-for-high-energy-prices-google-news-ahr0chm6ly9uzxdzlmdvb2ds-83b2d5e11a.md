## FAQ

**What are the main causes of high energy prices?**

High energy prices are primarily driven by increased demand, supply chain disruptions, geopolitical tensions, and the transition to renewable energy sources.

**How can individuals reduce their energy costs?**

Individuals can lower energy costs by adopting energy-efficient appliances, implementing smart home technologies, and being mindful of energy consumption habits.
