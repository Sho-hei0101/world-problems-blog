## FAQ

**What is geothermal energy?**

Geothermal energy harnesses heat from the Earth to provide heating and cooling solutions, using the stable temperature below the surface.

**How does a geothermal heating and cooling system work?**

It circulates a fluid through underground pipes to exchange heat with the ground, providing efficient heating in winter and cooling in summer.

**What are the cost benefits of geothermal systems?**

While initial installation costs can be high, geothermal systems typically lead to significant savings on energy bills in the long run.

**Are there any environmental impacts?**

Geothermal systems have a low environmental impact compared to fossil fuels, significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions and water usage.

**Can geothermal systems be installed in existing buildings?**

Yes, geothermal systems can often be retrofitted into existing buildings, although the feasibility depends on site-specific conditions.
