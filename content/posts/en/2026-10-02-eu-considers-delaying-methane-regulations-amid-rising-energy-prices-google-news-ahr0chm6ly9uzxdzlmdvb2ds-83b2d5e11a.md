## FAQ

**What are methane regulations?**

Methane regulations are policies aimed at reducing methane emissions from various sources, including agriculture, oil and gas, and waste management.

**Why is methane a concern for climate change?**

Methane is a potent greenhouse gas that is significantly more effective at trapping heat in the atmosphere than carbon dioxide, making it a critical target for climate action.

**What impact could delaying methane regulations have?**

Delaying these regulations could hinder progress toward climate goals and allow for continued high levels of methane emissions, exacerbating climate change.

**How do rising energy prices affect environmental policies?**

Rising energy prices can lead to resistance against strict environmental regulations, as stakeholders may prioritize economic stability over sustainability.

**What can individuals do to reduce methane emissions?**

Individuals can reduce methane emissions by minimizing food waste, supporting sustainable agricultural practices, and advocating for cleaner energy sources.
