## Practical actions you can take

If you're concerned about rising mortgage rates, there are several practical steps you can take to mitigate their impact. From refinancing options to government programs, understanding your choices can empower you to make informed decisions about your housing situation.

- Research current mortgage rates and trends in your area.
- Explore refinancing options to secure a lower rate.
- Consider fixed-rate mortgages for long-term stability.
- Stay informed about government programs that assist homebuyers.
- Assess your budget to accommodate potential rate increases.
- Consult with a financial advisor for personalized strategies.
- Join local housing advocacy groups for support and resources.
- Regularly review your mortgage terms and conditions.

**How do mortgage rates affect homebuyers?**
