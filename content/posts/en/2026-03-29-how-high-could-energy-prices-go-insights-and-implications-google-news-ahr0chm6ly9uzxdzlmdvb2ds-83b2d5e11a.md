## Practical actions you can take

To mitigate the impact of rising energy prices, individuals and businesses can adopt several practical strategies. These actions not only help manage costs but also promote sustainability and energy efficiency in the long run.

- Monitor energy consumption regularly.
- Invest in energy-efficient appliances and technologies.
- Explore renewable energy options such as solar or wind.
- Stay informed about market trends and geopolitical developments.
- Consider joining energy co-ops or community solar programs.
- Create a budget that accounts for potential energy cost increases.
- Advocate for policies that promote energy sustainability.
- Educate yourself and others about energy-saving practices.
