## FAQ

**What are solar panels?**

Solar panels are devices that convert sunlight into electricity, providing a renewable energy source for homes and businesses.

**How do heat pumps work?**

Heat pumps transfer heat from one place to another, using electricity to move heat into or out of a building, effectively heating or cooling spaces.

**Are there financial incentives for installing solar panels?**

Yes, many governments offer tax credits, rebates, and grants to encourage the installation of solar panels and heat pumps.

**Can solar panels reduce my electricity bill?**

Absolutely! By generating your own electricity, you can significantly lower your monthly energy costs and even earn credits for excess energy produced.

**Is it worth investing in a heat pump?**

Yes, heat pumps are energy-efficient and can provide substantial savings on heating and cooling costs, especially in moderate climates.
