## FAQ

**What causes energy prices to rise?**

Energy prices can rise due to various factors, including supply chain disruptions, geopolitical tensions, and increased demand.

**How does the Federal Reserve decide to raise interest rates?**

The Federal Reserve considers various economic indicators, including inflation rates, employment data, and overall economic growth before deciding to raise interest rates.
