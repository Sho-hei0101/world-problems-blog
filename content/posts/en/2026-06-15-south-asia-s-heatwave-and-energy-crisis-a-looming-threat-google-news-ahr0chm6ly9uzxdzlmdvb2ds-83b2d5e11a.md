## Problem overview

South Asia is currently experiencing severe heatwaves that have led to a significant energy crisis. High temperatures have increased electricity demand, particularly for cooling, while simultaneously straining energy supplies. The situation is exacerbated by aging infrastructure, inadequate energy policies, and the impacts of climate change. This dual crisis threatens public health, disrupts agriculture, and poses risks to economic stability across the region.

## FAQ

**What are the main causes of the heatwave in South Asia?**

The heatwave is primarily caused by climate change, which leads to increased temperatures and extreme weather patterns.
