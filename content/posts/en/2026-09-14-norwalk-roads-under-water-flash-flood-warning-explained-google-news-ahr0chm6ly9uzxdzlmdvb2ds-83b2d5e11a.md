## FAQ

**What should I do if I receive a flash flood warning?**

Seek higher ground immediately and avoid driving through flooded areas.

**How can I prepare my home for potential flooding?**

Consider elevating electrical appliances, sealing basement walls, and creating an emergency kit.

**What are the signs of flash flooding?**

Rapidly rising water, debris flow, and heavy rainfall can all indicate flash flooding.

**Is it safe to walk through floodwaters?**

No, floodwaters can be contaminated and hide dangerous debris.

**How can communities improve flood resilience?**

Investing in better drainage systems, early warning systems, and public education can enhance resilience.
