## FAQ

**What causes inflation to remain steady despite falling energy prices?**

Several factors, including demand for goods and services, labor costs, and monetary policy, can influence inflation rates.

**How does inflation affect my purchasing power?**

As inflation rises, the value of money decreases, meaning you can buy less with the same amount of money.

**What can consumers do to cope with inflation?**

Consumers can budget more effectively, seek discounts, and invest in inflation-protected assets.

**Is a 3% inflation rate considered high?**

A 3% inflation rate is generally viewed as moderate; however, it can still impact savings and spending habits.

**How can businesses prepare for potential inflation changes?**

Businesses can review pricing strategies, control costs, and adjust inventory to respond to inflation effectively.
