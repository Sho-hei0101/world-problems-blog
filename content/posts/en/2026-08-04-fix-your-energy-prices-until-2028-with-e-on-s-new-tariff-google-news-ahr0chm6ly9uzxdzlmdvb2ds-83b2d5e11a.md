## FAQ

**What is a fixed energy tariff?**

A fixed energy tariff locks in your energy prices for a set period, protecting you from future price increases.

**How do I know if this tariff is right for me?**

Consider your energy usage patterns, budget, and how long you plan to stay in your current home.

**Are there any fees associated with switching to a fixed tariff?**

Some suppliers may charge exit fees if you switch before the contract ends. Always check the terms before committing.

**Can I switch back to a variable tariff later?**

Yes, but be aware that switching back may expose you to fluctuating prices.

**What happens if energy prices fall while I'm on a fixed tariff?**

You will continue to pay the fixed rate you locked in, even if market prices drop.
