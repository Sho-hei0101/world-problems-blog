## FAQ

**What is the underground cash economy?**

The underground cash economy refers to economic activities that occur outside of formal regulations and tax compliance, often involving cash transactions to evade taxes.

**Why is tax evasion a concern?**

Tax evasion undermines government revenue, leading to reduced funding for public services and increasing inequality in society.
