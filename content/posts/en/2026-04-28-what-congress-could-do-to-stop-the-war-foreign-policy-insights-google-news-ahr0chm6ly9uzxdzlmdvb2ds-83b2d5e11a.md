## FAQ

**What powers does Congress have regarding foreign policy?**

Congress has the authority to declare war, regulate commerce with foreign nations, and approve treaties, which allows it to significantly influence foreign policy.

**How can citizens influence Congress on foreign policy matters?**

Citizens can engage with their representatives, participate in advocacy groups, and raise awareness through social media to influence foreign policy decisions.

**What are some examples of Congressional actions to promote peace?**

Congress can propose legislation to limit military funding, support diplomatic initiatives, and advocate for humanitarian aid in conflict zones.

**Why is Congressional involvement in foreign policy important?**

Congressional involvement ensures that various perspectives are considered, promoting a more democratic approach to foreign policy that reflects the will of the people.
