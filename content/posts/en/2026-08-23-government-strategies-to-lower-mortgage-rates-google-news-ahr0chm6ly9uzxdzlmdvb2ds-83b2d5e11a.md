## Problem overview

Mortgage rates have a significant impact on housing affordability, affecting millions of potential homebuyers. When rates are high, monthly payments increase, making it harder for families to purchase homes. This can lead to a slowdown in the housing market, reduced consumer confidence, and a wider economic impact. Understanding how government actions can influence these rates is essential for both policymakers and consumers alike.

## Why this matters globally

Lower mortgage rates can stimulate the housing market, making homeownership accessible to more people. This has a ripple effect on the economy, as increased home buying can lead to more jobs in construction, retail, and services. Globally, many countries look to their governments for solutions to housing affordability issues, making this a pressing concern that transcends borders.
