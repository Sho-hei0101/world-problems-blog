## Problem overview

The ongoing conflict in Iran has led to significant disruptions in the energy market, resulting in soaring oil and gas prices. These price hikes have a cascading effect, leading to increased inflation rates worldwide. As energy costs rise, consumers are feeling the pinch in their everyday lives, from higher gas prices to increased costs of goods and services. This situation poses a critical challenge for governments and economists as they strive to manage inflationary pressures while ensuring economic stability.

The implications of rising energy prices and inflation extend beyond national borders.
