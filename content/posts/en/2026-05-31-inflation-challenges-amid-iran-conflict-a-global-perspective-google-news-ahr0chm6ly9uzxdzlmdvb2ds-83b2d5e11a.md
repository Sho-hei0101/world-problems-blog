## FAQ

**What is the Fed's preferred inflation gauge?**

The Fed's preferred inflation gauge is the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index, which measures changes in the price of goods and services consumed by households.

**How does the Iran conflict affect global food prices?**

The Iran conflict disrupts supply chains and increases uncertainty in oil markets, leading to higher transportation costs and, consequently, increased food prices.

**What can consumers do to manage rising costs?**

Consumers can budget carefully, seek cost-effective alternatives, and stay informed about price trends to make better purchasing decisions.

**Why is inflation a concern for economies?**

Inflation erodes purchasing power, can lead to higher interest rates, and may slow down economic growth, impacting overall stability.

**Are there any long-term solutions to inflation?**

Long-term solutions include improving supply chain resilience, investing in renewable energy, and implementing policies that promote economic stability.
