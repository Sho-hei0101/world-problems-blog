To effectively manage the challenges posed by rising mortgage rates, individuals can take several proactive steps. Here are some practical actions to consider:

## FAQ

**Why are mortgage rates rising in the UK?**

Mortgage rates are rising primarily due to increasing inflation and higher energy prices, which influence borrowing costs.

**How does rising inflation affect mortgage rates?**

Rising inflation often leads to higher interest rates as lenders adjust rates to maintain their profit margins and manage risk.
