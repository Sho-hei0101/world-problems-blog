## FAQ

**What is a reverse mortgage?**

A reverse mortgage is a loan that allows homeowners to convert part of their home equity into cash, typically for retirement expenses.

**Are reverse mortgages considered income?**

No, funds received from a reverse mortgage are not considered taxable income.

**Will tax laws change in 2026 affecting reverse mortgages?**

While current laws state that reverse mortgages are not tax deductible, future legislation could potentially alter this.

**What should I consider before taking out a reverse mortgage?**

Consider your financial situation, potential fees, and how it may affect your heirs.

**Can I deduct interest on a reverse mortgage?**

Generally, interest on a reverse mortgage is not deductible until the loan is repaid.
