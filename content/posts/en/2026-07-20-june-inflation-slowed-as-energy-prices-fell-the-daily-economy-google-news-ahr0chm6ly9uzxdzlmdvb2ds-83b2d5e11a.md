## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation is typically caused by increased demand for goods and services, rising production costs, or monetary policy decisions by central banks.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Energy prices are a significant component of overall economic costs. When energy prices rise, it often leads to increased costs for goods and services, contributing to inflation.

**What can consumers do to protect themselves from inflation?**

Consumers can budget effectively, prioritize essential purchases, and consider investments that typically outpace inflation, such as stocks or real estate.

**Is inflation expected to continue slowing?**

While recent trends indicate a slowdown, future inflation rates depend on various factors, including energy prices, consumer demand, and global economic conditions.
