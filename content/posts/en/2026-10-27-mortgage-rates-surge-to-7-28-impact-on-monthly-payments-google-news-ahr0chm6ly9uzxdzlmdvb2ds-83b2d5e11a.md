## Practical actions you can take

For those affected by rising mortgage rates, there are several practical actions you can take to mitigate the impact on your finances and home-buying journey. Here are some steps to consider:

- Improve your credit score to qualify for better rates.
- Consult with a financial advisor for tailored advice.

- Consider alternative financing options if traditional mortgages are unaffordable.
- Evaluate the timing of your home purchase based on market conditions.
