## Problem overview

The War on Terror, initiated in response to the September 11 attacks in 2001, represents a broad military, political, and ideological campaign aimed at combating terrorism. This conflict has involved numerous nations and has led to significant military engagements, changes in foreign policy, and debates about civil liberties and human rights. The implications of this war extend beyond the battlefield, influencing global security dynamics and international relations.

## Why this matters globally

The War on Terror matters globally as it has redefined state security policies, led to the establishment of military bases around the world, and influenced international cooperation against terrorism. It has also sparked discussions about the balance between national security and civil liberties, making it a relevant topic for citizens and policymakers alike. Understanding this conflict is essential for grasping current geopolitical tensions and for anticipating future international relations.
