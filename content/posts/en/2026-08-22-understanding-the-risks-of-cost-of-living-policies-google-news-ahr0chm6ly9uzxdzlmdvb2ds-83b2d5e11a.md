The challenge lies in balancing immediate relief with long-term economic stability.

## FAQ

**What are cost of living policies?**

Cost of living policies are measures implemented by governments to assist individuals and families facing high living expenses.

**Why are these policies important?**

They aim to provide financial relief to households, especially during times of economic hardship.

**What are some risks associated with cost of living policies?**
