## FAQ

**What are the main causes of water scarcity in California?**

The primary causes include prolonged droughts, climate change, and increased demand for water from agriculture and urban areas.

**How can water storage improve California's resilience to climate change?**

Water storage helps to capture and retain water during wet periods, ensuring a reliable supply during dry spells and reducing the impact of drought.

**What are some examples of water storage solutions?**

Examples include building new reservoirs, improving groundwater recharge methods, and utilizing rainwater harvesting systems.

**How can individuals contribute to water conservation efforts?**

Individuals can reduce water usage at home, fix leaks, and support local policies aimed at improving water management.

**What role does agriculture play in California's water use?**

Agriculture accounts for a significant portion of California's water use, making efficient irrigation practices and water management essential for sustainability.
