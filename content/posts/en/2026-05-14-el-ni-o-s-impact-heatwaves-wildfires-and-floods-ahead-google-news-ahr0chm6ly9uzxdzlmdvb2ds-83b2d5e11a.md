## Problem overview

The El Niño phenomenon is a climate pattern that occurs periodically, characterized by the warming of ocean surface temperatures in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean. This warming can trigger a cascade of extreme weather events across the globe, including intense heatwaves, devastating wildfires, and catastrophic floods. As scientists predict the onset of a strong El Niño, communities worldwide must brace for the potential impacts that could disrupt ecosystems, economies, and daily life.
