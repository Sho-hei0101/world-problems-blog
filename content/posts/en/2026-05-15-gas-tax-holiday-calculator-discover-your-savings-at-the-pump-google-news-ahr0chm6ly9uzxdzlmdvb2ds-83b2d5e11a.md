## FAQ

**What is a gas tax holiday?**

A gas tax holiday is a temporary suspension of fuel taxes, aimed at reducing gas prices for consumers.

**How does the Gas Tax Holiday Calculator work?**

**Are gas tax holidays effective in reducing fuel costs?**
