## Practical actions you can take

To combat the growing threat of wildfires, individuals, communities, and policymakers can take several practical actions. Here are some steps that can be implemented to help mitigate the risks and prepare for future wildfire events.

- Create defensible space around your home by removing flammable materials.
- Develop and practice an emergency evacuation plan with your family.
- Stay informed about local fire conditions and alerts.
- Participate in community fire prevention programs.
- Support policies that promote sustainable land management.
- Educate others about wildfire risks and prevention strategies.
- Advocate for investment in firefighting resources and training.
- Engage with local government initiatives aimed at wildfire preparedness.

## FAQ

**What are the main causes of wildfires?**

Wildfires can be caused by natural events like lightning or human activities such as campfires, discarded cigarettes, and power lines.

**How can communities prepare for wildfires?**

Communities can prepare by creating firebreaks, conducting controlled burns, and implementing early warning systems.

**What role does climate change play in wildfires?**

Climate change contributes to extreme weather conditions, leading to hotter temperatures and drier environments, which increase wildfire risks.

**How can individuals reduce wildfire risks?**

Individuals can help by maintaining defensible space around their homes, being cautious with fire use, and staying informed about local fire conditions.

**What are the economic impacts of wildfires?**

Wildfires can lead to significant economic losses in property damage, healthcare costs due to smoke inhalation, and impacts on local economies dependent on tourism and agriculture.
