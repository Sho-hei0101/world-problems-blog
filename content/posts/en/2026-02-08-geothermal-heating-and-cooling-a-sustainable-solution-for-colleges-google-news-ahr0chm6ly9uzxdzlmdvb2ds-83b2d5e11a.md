## FAQ

**What is geothermal heating and cooling?**

Geothermal heating and cooling involves using the earth's stable underground temperature to heat or cool buildings, providing an energy-efficient alternative to traditional HVAC systems.

**How does geothermal energy save money?**

Geothermal systems have lower operating costs due to their efficiency, often resulting in significant savings on energy bills over time.

**What are the environmental benefits of geothermal systems?**

These systems significantly reduce greenhouse gas emissions and conserve water, making them an eco-friendly choice for heating and cooling.

**Can any building use geothermal heating and cooling?**

While most buildings can benefit from geothermal systems, installation feasibility may depend on site conditions and local regulations.

**How long does it take to install a geothermal system?**

Installation times vary, but typically, it can take a few weeks to several months depending on the size of the system and site conditions.
