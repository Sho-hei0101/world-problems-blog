## FAQ

**How do geopolitical events affect mortgage rates?**

Geopolitical events can create uncertainty, leading to fluctuations in investor confidence and interest rates.

**What should I do if I need to buy a home during a conflict?**

Stay informed about market trends, consult with financial advisors, and consider locking in rates if they are favorable.
