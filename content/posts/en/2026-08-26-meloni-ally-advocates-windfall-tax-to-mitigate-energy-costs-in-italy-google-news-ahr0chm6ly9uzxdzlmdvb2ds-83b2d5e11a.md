## FAQ

**What is a windfall tax?**

A windfall tax is a one-time tax imposed on companies that have unexpectedly high profits, often during economic crises, to redistribute wealth.

**How would a windfall tax benefit consumers?**

The revenue generated from a windfall tax could be used to subsidize energy costs for consumers, easing their financial burden.

**Are there examples of windfall taxes implemented in other countries?**

Yes, countries like the UK have implemented windfall taxes during crises to address economic inequalities and support struggling consumers.

**What are the potential drawbacks of a windfall tax?**

Critics argue that windfall taxes could discourage investment in the energy sector and may lead to reduced production if companies anticipate lower profits.

**How can individuals prepare for rising energy prices?**

Individuals can reduce energy consumption, explore alternative energy sources, and stay informed about government support programs.
