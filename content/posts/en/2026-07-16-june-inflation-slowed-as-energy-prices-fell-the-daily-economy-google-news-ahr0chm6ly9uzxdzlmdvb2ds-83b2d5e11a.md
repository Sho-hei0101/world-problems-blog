## FAQ

**What causes inflation to rise?**

Inflation can rise due to increased demand for goods and services, higher production costs, or expansionary monetary policies.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Energy prices directly influence transportation and production costs, which can lead to higher prices for goods and services.

**What can consumers do to protect themselves from inflation?**

Consumers can budget wisely, invest in inflation-resistant assets, and stay informed about economic trends.

**Is a decrease in inflation always good?**

While a decrease in inflation can indicate economic stability, it may also signal weak demand or economic slowdown.

**How often do inflation rates change?**

Inflation rates can change monthly based on economic conditions, consumer behavior, and market dynamics.
