## Problem overview

The Middle East has long been a critical hub for global energy supply, particularly oil and gas. Ongoing geopolitical tensions and crises in the region can lead to significant fluctuations in energy prices. As the EU heavily relies on energy imports, especially from the Middle East, a prolonged crisis could have dire consequences for the European economy. This situation warrants a thorough examination to understand the potential impacts and necessary responses.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of a Middle East crisis extend beyond regional borders, affecting global energy markets and economic stability. As energy prices rise due to supply disruptions, countries dependent on these resources, like those in the EU, face inflationary pressures, reduced economic growth, and increased energy poverty among consumers. The interconnected nature of global trade means that shocks in one region can ripple through the global economy, impacting everything from consumer prices to national security.
