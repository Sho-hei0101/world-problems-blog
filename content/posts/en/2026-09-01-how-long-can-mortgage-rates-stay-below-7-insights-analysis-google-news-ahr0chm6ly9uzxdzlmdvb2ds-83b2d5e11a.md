## FAQ

**What factors influence mortgage rates?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by various factors including economic indicators, inflation, and Federal Reserve policies.

**How do I know if it's a good time to buy a home?**

Consider current mortgage rates, your financial situation, and market conditions. Consulting with a financial advisor can also help.

**What should I do if rates rise above 7%?**

If rates rise, consider locking in a rate if you’re in the process of buying or refinancing, and evaluate your budget to adjust for higher costs.

**Can I refinance my mortgage if rates go down?**

Yes, refinancing is a common strategy to take advantage of lower rates, which can reduce monthly payments and overall interest costs.

**What impact do low mortgage rates have on the housing market?**

Low rates typically increase buyer demand, which can drive up home prices and stimulate construction and related industries.
