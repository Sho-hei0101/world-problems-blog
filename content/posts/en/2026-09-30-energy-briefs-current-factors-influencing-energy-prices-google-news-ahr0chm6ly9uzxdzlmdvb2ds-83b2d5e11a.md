## FAQ

**What are the main factors affecting energy prices?**

Key factors include supply and demand dynamics, geopolitical events, weather conditions, and regulatory changes.

**How can I reduce my energy costs?**

Consider energy-efficient appliances, adjust usage patterns, and explore alternative energy sources.
