## FAQ

**What is CPI and why is it important?**

CPI stands for Consumer Price Index. It measures the average change over time in the prices paid by consumers for goods and services, serving as an indicator of inflation.

**How does the Iran conflict affect global energy prices?**

Geopolitical tensions, such as the Iran conflict, can disrupt oil supply chains and create uncertainty in the markets, leading to increased prices.

**What can individuals do to protect themselves from inflation?**

Individuals can consider budgeting more effectively, investing in inflation-resistant assets, and exploring alternative energy sources to mitigate rising costs.
