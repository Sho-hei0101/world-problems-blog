## FAQ

**What is the 30% rule for housing?**

The 30% rule suggests that individuals should spend no more than 30% of their gross income on housing costs, including rent or mortgage payments.

**How can I lower my rent or mortgage payments?**

You can lower your payments by negotiating with your landlord, refinancing your mortgage, or considering a less expensive housing option.

**What should I do if housing costs exceed my budget?**

If housing costs exceed your budget, consider reducing discretionary spending, seeking additional income sources, or exploring more affordable housing options.

**Are there tools available to help budget for housing?**

Yes, there are various budgeting apps and financial planning tools that can help you track expenses and plan for housing costs.

**Is it better to rent or buy a home?**

Whether to rent or buy depends on your personal financial situation, market conditions, and long-term goals. Each option has its pros and cons.
