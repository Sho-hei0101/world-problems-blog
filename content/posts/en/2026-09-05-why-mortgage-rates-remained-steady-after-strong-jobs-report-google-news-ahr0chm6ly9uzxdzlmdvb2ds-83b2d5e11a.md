## FAQ

**Why did mortgage rates not rise after a strong jobs report?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by a variety of factors, including inflation, Federal Reserve policies, and overall economic sentiment, not just employment numbers.

**What should homebuyers do in a stable mortgage rate environment?**

Homebuyers should assess their financial situation and consider locking in rates if they find a favorable mortgage option.
