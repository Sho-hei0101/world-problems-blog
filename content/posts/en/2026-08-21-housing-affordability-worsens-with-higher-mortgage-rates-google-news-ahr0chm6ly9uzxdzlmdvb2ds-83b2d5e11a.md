## Problem overview

As mortgage rates continue to rise, housing affordability is becoming a significant challenge for many individuals and families. The cost of borrowing has increased, leading to higher monthly payments for prospective homebuyers. This situation is pushing many potential buyers out of the market, resulting in a substantial impact on both the housing market and overall economic stability. With fewer people able to afford homes, the dynamics of supply and demand are shifting, exacerbating the affordability crisis.

## Why this matters globally

Housing affordability is a critical issue that affects individuals and families globally. When people cannot afford housing, it can lead to increased homelessness, economic instability, and social unrest. The consequences of high mortgage rates extend beyond individual homeowners; they can impact local economies, real estate markets, and even government policies. Understanding the broader implications is essential for grasping the significance of the current housing crisis.

To navigate the current housing market effectively, it's crucial to take practical actions. Here are some strategies to consider:
