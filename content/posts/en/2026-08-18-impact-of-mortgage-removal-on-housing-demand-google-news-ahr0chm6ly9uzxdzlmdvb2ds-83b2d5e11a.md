## Problem overview

The traditional mortgage system has long been a cornerstone of home buying, enabling millions to purchase homes. However, as housing prices soar and economic disparities widen, the feasibility of homeownership is increasingly questioned. Removing the mortgage mechanism could radically alter the landscape of housing demand, making homeownership more accessible for some while potentially destabilizing markets reliant on mortgage financing.

## FAQ

**What would happen to housing prices if mortgages were removed?**

Housing prices may stabilize or decrease as more buyers could afford homes without the burden of mortgage payments.

**How would the removal of mortgages affect first-time homebuyers?**

First-time homebuyers could find it easier to enter the market, as they would not need to secure financing.

**What alternative financing methods might emerge?**

New models such as community land trusts, rent-to-own agreements, and cooperative housing could gain traction.

**Could this change impact the rental market?**

Yes, a shift in homeownership dynamics may lead to increased demand for rental properties if fewer people can buy homes.

**What are the potential risks of removing mortgages?**

The risks include market instability and potential financial strain on banks that rely on mortgage lending as a primary income source.
