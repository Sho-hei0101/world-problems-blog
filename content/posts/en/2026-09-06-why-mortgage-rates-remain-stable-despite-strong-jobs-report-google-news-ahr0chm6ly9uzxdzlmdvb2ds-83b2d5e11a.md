## FAQ

**Why did mortgage rates not change after the jobs report?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by various factors, including market expectations, inflation, and the overall economic climate, which can sometimes dampen the immediate effects of strong job growth.

**How often do mortgage rates fluctuate?**

Mortgage rates can fluctuate daily based on economic indicators, market conditions, and investor sentiment.

**What should homebuyers do in a stable rate environment?**

Homebuyers should assess their financial situation, consider fixed vs. adjustable rates, and consult with real estate professionals to make informed decisions.

**Are there risks in waiting for lower mortgage rates?**

Yes, waiting for lower rates can lead to missed opportunities, especially if housing prices continue to rise or if rates stabilize or increase.

**What other factors should I consider when applying for a mortgage?**

Consider your credit score, down payment, loan terms, and the overall economic outlook, which can all affect your mortgage options.
