## Problem overview

The ongoing conflict in Iran has led to significant disruptions in energy supply chains, resulting in soaring energy prices. This spike is contributing to a rise in inflation rates, reaching a three-year high in several countries. Consumers are facing increased costs for essential goods and services, which can strain household budgets and impact economic stability.

## Why this matters globally

This situation is critical on a global scale as energy prices influence various sectors, from transportation to manufacturing.
