## FAQ

**What causes mortgage rates to rise?**

Mortgage rates typically rise due to inflation concerns, changes in the Federal Reserve's monetary policy, and overall economic conditions.

**How can rising mortgage rates affect homebuyers?**

Rising mortgage rates can increase monthly payments, making homes less affordable and potentially slowing down the housing market.
