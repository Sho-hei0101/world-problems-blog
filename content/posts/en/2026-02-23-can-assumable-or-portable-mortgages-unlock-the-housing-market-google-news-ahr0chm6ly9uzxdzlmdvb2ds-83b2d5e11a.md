## FAQ

**What is an assumable mortgage?**

An assumable mortgage allows a buyer to take over the seller's existing mortgage, often at a lower interest rate.

**What is a portable mortgage?**

A portable mortgage allows borrowers to transfer their existing mortgage to a new property without incurring penalties.

**How do these mortgages benefit buyers?**

They can offer lower interest rates and reduce closing costs, making housing more affordable.

**Are there risks associated with assumable or portable mortgages?**

Yes, potential risks include the original borrower's creditworthiness and the terms of the existing mortgage.

**How can I find out if a mortgage is assumable or portable?**

Check with your lender or review your mortgage documents to see if these options are available.
