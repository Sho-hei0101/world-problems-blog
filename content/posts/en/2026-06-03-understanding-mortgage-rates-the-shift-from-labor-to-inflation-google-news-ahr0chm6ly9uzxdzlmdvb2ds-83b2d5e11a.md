**What are the main factors currently influencing mortgage rates?**

Mortgage rates are primarily influenced by inflation rates, economic growth, and Federal Reserve policies.

**How does inflation impact my mortgage rates?**

As inflation rises, central banks may increase interest rates to control it, leading to higher mortgage rates.
