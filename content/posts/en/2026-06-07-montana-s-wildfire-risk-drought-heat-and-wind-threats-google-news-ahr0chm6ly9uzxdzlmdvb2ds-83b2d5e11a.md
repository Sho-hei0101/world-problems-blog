## Practical actions you can take

Taking proactive steps can significantly reduce the risks associated with wildfires. Here are some practical actions individuals and communities can implement:

- Create a defensible space around your property by clearing flammable debris.
- Use fire-resistant materials for home construction and landscaping.
- Develop an emergency plan for your family, including evacuation routes.
- Stay informed about local fire conditions through alerts and news.
- Participate in community fire prevention programs and workshops.
- Prepare an emergency kit with essentials for at least 72 hours.
- Regularly maintain landscaping and remove dead vegetation.
- Educate yourself and your neighbors about wildfire risks and safety.

## FAQ

**What causes wildfires?**

Wildfires are primarily caused by natural factors such as lightning strikes, but human activities like campfires, discarded cigarettes, and power lines can also ignite fires.

**How can I prepare my home for wildfire season?**

Create a defensible space around your home by clearing flammable materials, using fire-resistant building materials, and maintaining a well-watered landscape.

**What should I do if a wildfire is approaching?**

Stay informed through local news, follow evacuation orders, and have an emergency kit ready with essentials like food, water, medications, and important documents.

**How does climate change affect wildfire risk?**

Climate change leads to higher temperatures and altered precipitation patterns, resulting in drier conditions that increase the likelihood and severity of wildfires.

**Are there any community programs for wildfire preparedness?**

Many local governments and organizations offer programs to educate residents about wildfire risks and preparedness strategies, including workshops and resources.
