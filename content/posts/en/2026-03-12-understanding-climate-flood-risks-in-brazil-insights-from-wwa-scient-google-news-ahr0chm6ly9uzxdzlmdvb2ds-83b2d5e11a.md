## FAQ

**What causes flooding in Brazil?**

Flooding in Brazil is primarily caused by heavy rainfall, deforestation, and climate change, which exacerbates extreme weather events.

**How can communities prepare for flooding?**

Communities can prepare by developing emergency plans, investing in flood defenses, and promoting sustainable land use practices.

**What role does deforestation play in flooding?**

Deforestation reduces the land's ability to absorb rainfall, leading to increased runoff and a higher risk of flooding.

**Is climate change the only factor affecting flood risks?**

While climate change is a significant factor, urban planning, land use, and environmental degradation also contribute to flood risks.

**How can individuals help mitigate climate change?**

Individuals can reduce their carbon footprint by using public transport, conserving energy, supporting renewable energy initiatives, and advocating for climate policies.
