## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation can be caused by various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, supply chain disruptions, and rising production costs.

**How does inflation affect my purchasing power?**

As prices rise, the value of money decreases, meaning you can buy less with the same amount of money, effectively reducing your purchasing power.

**What can I do to protect my finances from inflation?**

Consider budgeting, investing in assets that typically appreciate, and exploring ways to increase your income to counteract inflation's effects.

**Is inflation expected to continue rising?**

While predictions vary, many economists suggest that inflation may remain elevated due to ongoing supply chain issues and increased demand.

**How does inflation impact the housing market?**

Inflation can lead to higher mortgage rates and increased housing prices, making it more challenging for individuals to purchase homes.
