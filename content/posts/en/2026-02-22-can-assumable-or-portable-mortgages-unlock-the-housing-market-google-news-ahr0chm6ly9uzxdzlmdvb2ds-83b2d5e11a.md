## FAQ

**What is an assumable mortgage?**

An assumable mortgage allows a buyer to take over the existing mortgage of the seller, potentially benefiting from lower interest rates.

**What is a portable mortgage?**

A portable mortgage enables homeowners to transfer their existing mortgage to a new property, maintaining their current interest rate.

**How can these mortgages help in a rising interest rate environment?**

By allowing buyers to assume lower-rate mortgages, these options can make home purchases more affordable despite rising rates.

**Are there any risks associated with assumable or portable mortgages?**

Yes, risks include potential fees, the need for lender approval, and the possibility of taking on the seller's debt obligations.

**How can I find homes with assumable mortgages?**

Real estate agents can help identify properties with assumable mortgages, and online listings may indicate this feature.
