## FAQ

**What is the Consumer Price Index (CPI)?**

The CPI measures the average change over time in the prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services. It is a key indicator of inflation.

**Why is headline inflation important?**

Headline inflation reflects the total inflation within an economy, influencing monetary policy, investment strategies, and consumer behavior.

**How do rising housing and energy costs affect consumers?**

Higher housing and energy costs can reduce disposable income, leading consumers to cut back on other spending, which can slow economic growth.

**What steps can consumers take in response to rising costs?**

Consumers can budget more carefully, seek energy-efficient solutions, and consider alternative housing options to manage rising expenses.

**Is inflation expected to rise further?**

While predictions vary, many economists analyze current trends to forecast inflation, but it is influenced by various unpredictable factors.
