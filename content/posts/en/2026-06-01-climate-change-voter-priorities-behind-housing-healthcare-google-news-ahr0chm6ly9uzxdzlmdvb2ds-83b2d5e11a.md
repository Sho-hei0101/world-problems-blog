## Problem overview

Recent polls indicate that climate change is not a top priority for voters, who rank housing, healthcare, and the cost of living significantly higher. This trend raises concerns about the future of climate policy and action as immediate needs overshadow long-term environmental issues. As voters focus on urgent economic and social issues, climate change could be sidelined, leading to insufficient political pressure for necessary action.

## Why this matters globally

This issue is significant on a global scale as climate change poses a threat to ecosystems, economies, and human health. While immediate concerns like housing and healthcare are pressing, neglecting climate change can exacerbate these problems in the long run. For instance, extreme weather events can damage homes and affect health through pollution and disease. Understanding this dynamic is crucial for policymakers to develop comprehensive strategies that address both immediate needs and long-term sustainability.

## FAQ

**Why do voters prioritize housing and healthcare over climate change?**
