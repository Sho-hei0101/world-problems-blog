## FAQ

**What causes inflation to rise?**

Inflation can rise due to various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, higher production costs, and supply chain disruptions.

**How does inflation affect my daily expenses?**

Rising inflation leads to higher prices for essential goods and services, which can strain household budgets and reduce purchasing power.

**What can I do to protect my finances during inflation?**

Consider budgeting carefully, exploring investment options that typically outpace inflation, and reducing discretionary spending.

**Is inflation expected to continue rising?**

While predictions vary, ongoing economic conditions and policies will significantly influence future inflation trends.

**How does inflation impact interest rates?**

Typically, rising inflation can lead to higher interest rates as central banks may increase rates to control inflation.
