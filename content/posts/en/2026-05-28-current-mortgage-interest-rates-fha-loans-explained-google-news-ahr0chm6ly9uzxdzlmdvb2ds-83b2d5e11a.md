## FAQ

**What is the current average mortgage interest rate?**

The average mortgage interest rate can vary widely based on market conditions, credit scores, and loan types. It's best to check current rates with lenders.

**What are FHA loans?**

FHA loans are government-backed mortgages designed to help lower-income and first-time homebuyers qualify for home financing with lower down payments and credit score requirements.

**How do I qualify for an FHA loan?**

To qualify for an FHA loan, you typically need a credit score of at least 580 (for a 3.5% down payment) and a debt-to-income ratio of 43% or lower.

**Can I refinance an FHA loan?**

Yes, FHA loans can be refinanced through various programs, including the FHA Streamline Refinance, which simplifies the process for existing FHA borrowers.

**What are the benefits of FHA loans?**

FHA loans offer lower down payments, more flexible credit requirements, and competitive interest rates, making them accessible for many buyers.
