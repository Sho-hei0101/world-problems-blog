## FAQ

**What are the main causes of rising inflation?**

Rising inflation can be attributed to factors such as increased demand, supply chain disruptions, and higher costs of raw materials, particularly energy.

**How can consumers cope with higher prices?**

Consumers can adjust their budgets, seek discounts, and prioritize essential purchases while being mindful of their spending habits.

No, inflation rates can vary significantly between regions due to local economic conditions, government policies, and consumer behavior.
