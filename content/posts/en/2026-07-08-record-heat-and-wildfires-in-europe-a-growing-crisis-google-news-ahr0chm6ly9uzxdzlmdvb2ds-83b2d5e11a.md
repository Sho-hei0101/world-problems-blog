## FAQ

**What causes wildfires to spread rapidly?**

Wildfires spread rapidly due to dry conditions, high temperatures, and strong winds, which can create ideal conditions for fires to ignite and expand quickly.

**How can communities prepare for wildfires?**

Communities can prepare by creating defensible space around homes, developing evacuation plans, and establishing community alert systems.

**What role does climate change play in wildfires?**

Climate change contributes to more extreme weather conditions, leading to hotter, drier climates that increase the likelihood and severity of wildfires.

**Are wildfires only a problem in Europe?**

No, wildfires are a global issue, affecting regions worldwide, including the United States, Australia, and parts of Asia.

**What can individuals do to help prevent wildfires?**

Individuals can help prevent wildfires by following local fire regulations, reporting suspicious activities, and being cautious with outdoor activities that could start fires.
