## Problem overview

The ongoing conflict in Iran has led to a significant surge in energy prices, raising alarms at the European Central Bank (ECB) regarding potential inflation risks. As energy costs rise, they can have a cascading effect on various sectors of the economy, leading to higher consumer prices and diminished purchasing power. This situation poses a challenge not only for policymakers but also for consumers and businesses alike, as they navigate the implications of rising costs in their daily lives.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of the Iran war and the resultant energy price hikes extend beyond Europe; they resonate on a global scale.
