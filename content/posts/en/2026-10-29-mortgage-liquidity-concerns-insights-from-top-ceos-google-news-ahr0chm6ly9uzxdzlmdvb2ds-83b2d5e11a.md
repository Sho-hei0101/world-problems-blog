## FAQ

**What is mortgage liquidity?**

Mortgage liquidity refers to the ease with which mortgage loans can be bought and sold in the market. Higher liquidity means more available financing options.

**Why are CEOs concerned about mortgage liquidity?**

CEOs are concerned because low liquidity can lead to higher borrowing costs, reduced access to loans, and instability in the housing market.

**How does mortgage liquidity affect homebuyers?**

Lower liquidity can result in higher interest rates and fewer financing options, making it more challenging for homebuyers to secure loans.

**What actions can I take if I'm looking to buy a home?**

Stay informed about market trends, consider locking in rates early, and explore various financing options.

**Is the mortgage liquidity issue temporary?**

While it can be temporary, several factors influencing liquidity can persist, so it's important to remain vigilant.
