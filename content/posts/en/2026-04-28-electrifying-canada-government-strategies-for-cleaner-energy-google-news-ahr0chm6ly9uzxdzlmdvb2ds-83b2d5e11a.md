## Problem overview

As Canada moves towards a cleaner energy future, households face significant challenges in electrifying their homes. The transition to electric heating, cooking, and transportation is essential for reducing greenhouse gas emissions. However, many households lack the financial resources and information necessary to make this shift. Government action is crucial in providing support systems that facilitate this transition and ensure that clean energy is accessible and affordable for all Canadians.

## Why this matters globally

## FAQ

**What is electrification?**

Electrification refers to the process of converting systems that use fossil fuels to electric power, such as heating, cooking, and transportation.

**Why is electrification important?**

Electrification is crucial for reducing greenhouse gas emissions, promoting clean energy adoption, and achieving climate goals.

**How can governments support households in electrifying?**

Governments can provide financial incentives, subsidies, and educational resources to help households transition to electric energy solutions.

**What are some barriers to electrification?**

Barriers include high upfront costs, lack of information, and limited access to clean energy technologies in certain regions.

**What can households do to electrify?**

Households can explore available incentives, invest in electric appliances, and participate in local energy programs.
