## Problem overview

In recent months, UK lenders have been raising mortgage rates significantly. This trend has been largely driven by concerns over inflation and rising energy prices, which have created an uncertain economic environment. Homeowners and potential buyers are feeling the pressure as the cost of borrowing increases, potentially making homeownership less accessible for many.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of rising mortgage rates extend beyond individual homeowners; they affect the entire economy. Higher borrowing costs can lead to decreased consumer spending, which in turn may slow down economic growth. Additionally, as more households struggle with increased mortgage payments, there is a risk of an increase in defaults, which could further destabilize the housing market. Understanding these dynamics is essential for policymakers, lenders, and consumers alike.
