## FAQ

**What causes inflation in India?**

Inflation in India is primarily driven by rising food and energy prices, along with supply chain disruptions and increased demand.

**How does inflation affect everyday consumers?**

Inflation erodes purchasing power, making goods and services more expensive for consumers, which can lead to changes in spending habits.

**What can individuals do to prepare for inflation?**

Individuals can budget more carefully, consider investments that traditionally perform well during inflation, and explore cost-cutting measures.

**Is inflation a concern for the global economy?**

Yes, rising inflation in major economies like India can have widespread effects on global markets, trade, and economic policies.

**What are the long-term implications of sustained inflation?**

Sustained inflation can lead to economic instability, reduced consumer confidence, and potential changes in monetary policy.
