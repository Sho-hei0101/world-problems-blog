**What are autonomous surface craft?**

Autonomous surface craft are unmanned vehicles designed to operate on the water's surface, equipped with sensors and navigation systems to perform various tasks without human intervention.

**How do autonomous surface craft enhance maritime security?**

They provide continuous surveillance, can cover vast areas, and gather data in real-time, helping authorities respond more effectively to threats.
