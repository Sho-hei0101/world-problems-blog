## FAQ

**What is the 10-year yield?**

The 10-year yield refers to the return on investment for U.S. government bonds that mature in ten years. It's a key indicator of economic health and influences mortgage rates.

**How do mortgage rates affect home buying?**

Mortgage rates directly impact monthly payments. Lower rates can make homes more affordable, while higher rates can reduce buying power.

**What factors influence mortgage rates?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by various factors including the 10-year yield, inflation, Federal Reserve policies, and overall economic conditions.
