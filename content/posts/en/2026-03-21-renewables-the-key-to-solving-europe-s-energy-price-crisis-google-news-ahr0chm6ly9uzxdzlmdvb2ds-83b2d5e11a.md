## Problem overview

High energy prices have become a significant concern for consumers and businesses across Europe. The volatility in energy markets, exacerbated by geopolitical tensions and supply chain disruptions, has led to a pressing need for sustainable solutions. The European Council has identified renewable energy sources as a fundamental approach to addressing these challenges. By investing in renewables, Europe aims to create a more stable, affordable, and sustainable energy landscape.

## Why this matters globally

This issue is not confined to Europe; it has global ramifications. Energy prices impact economies worldwide, affecting everything from household budgets to international trade. Transitioning to renewable energy not only helps mitigate price volatility but also reduces reliance on fossil fuels, which is crucial for combating climate change. Furthermore, as countries collaborate on energy solutions, they pave the way for a more resilient global energy system that can withstand future shocks.
