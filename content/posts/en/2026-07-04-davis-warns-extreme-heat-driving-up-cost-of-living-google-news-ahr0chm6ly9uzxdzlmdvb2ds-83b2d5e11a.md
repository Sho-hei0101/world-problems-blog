## Practical actions you can take

To combat the rising costs associated with extreme heat, individuals and communities can take several practical actions. These measures not only help mitigate the impact on living expenses but also promote resilience against future climate challenges.

- Stay informed about local weather forecasts and heat advisories.
- Invest in energy-efficient appliances to reduce electricity bills.
- Consider growing your own fruits and vegetables.
- Participate in community discussions about climate resilience.
- Advocate for local policies that address climate change.
- Create a personal budget that factors in potential increases in living costs.
- Educate others about the impacts of extreme heat on living expenses.
- Explore government assistance programs for heat-related costs.

## Regional perspective

Governments in these regions must devise strategic responses to protect both the economy and public health.
