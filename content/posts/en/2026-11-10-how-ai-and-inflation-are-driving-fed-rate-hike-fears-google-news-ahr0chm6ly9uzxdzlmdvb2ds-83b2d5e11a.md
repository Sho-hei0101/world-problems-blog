## FAQ

**What is the Federal Reserve's role in the economy?**

The Federal Reserve manages the US monetary policy, including setting interest rates to promote economic stability.

**How does inflation affect interest rates?**

Typically, higher inflation leads the Federal Reserve to increase interest rates to control spending and stabilize prices.
