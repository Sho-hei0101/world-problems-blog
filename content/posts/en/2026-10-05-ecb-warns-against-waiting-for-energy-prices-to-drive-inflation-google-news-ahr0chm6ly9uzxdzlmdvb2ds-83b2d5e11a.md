The European Central Bank (ECB) has raised concerns that high energy prices could lead to broader inflationary pressures across the economy. With energy costs already rising, policymakers fear that waiting too long to address these issues may exacerbate economic instability. This situation creates a precarious balance where immediate action is necessary to prevent inflation from spiraling out of control.
