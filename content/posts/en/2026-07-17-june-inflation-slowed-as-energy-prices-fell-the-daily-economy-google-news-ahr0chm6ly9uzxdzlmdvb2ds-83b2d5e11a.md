## FAQ

**What is inflation?**

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises, eroding purchasing power.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Energy prices are a significant component of overall inflation; when they rise, other costs often follow, leading to higher inflation rates.

**What can individuals do to protect against inflation?**

Individuals can consider budgeting, investing in inflation-protected assets, and staying informed about economic trends.

**Is the slowdown in inflation a permanent trend?**

It is uncertain; while June showed a slowdown, various factors could influence inflation rates in the future.

**How do governments respond to rising inflation?**

Governments may implement monetary policies, such as adjusting interest rates, to control inflation.
