## FAQ

**What are the main factors influencing mortgage rates?**

Mortgage rates are influenced by inflation, economic growth, and the Federal Reserve's monetary policy.

**How do high mortgage rates affect the housing market?**

High mortgage rates can decrease home sales and increase demand for rentals, making homeownership less attainable.

**Can I refinance if mortgage rates are high?**

Refinancing during high mortgage rates may not be beneficial unless you have a significantly higher rate currently.

**What should potential homebuyers do in this market?**

Potential homebuyers should assess their financial situation, consider waiting for rate drops, and explore different financing options.

**How can I keep track of mortgage rate changes?**

You can track mortgage rates through financial news websites, banks, and mortgage lenders' websites.
