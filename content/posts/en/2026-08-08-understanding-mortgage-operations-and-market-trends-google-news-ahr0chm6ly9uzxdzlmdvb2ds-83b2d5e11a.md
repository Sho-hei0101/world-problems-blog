## FAQ

**What is the role of capital markets in mortgage operations?**

Capital markets provide the necessary funding for mortgage loans, allowing lenders to offer competitive rates to borrowers.

**How does weak job data affect the mortgage market?**

Weak job data can lead to decreased consumer confidence, resulting in fewer mortgage applications and potentially higher interest rates.

**What are flood certification tools?**

Flood certification tools help lenders assess the flood risk of properties, ensuring compliance with federal regulations.

**Why is quality control important in mortgage operations?**

Quality control ensures that loans meet regulatory standards and reduces the risk of defaults, protecting lenders and investors.
