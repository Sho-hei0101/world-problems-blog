## FAQ

**How does conflict affect food security?**

Conflict disrupts agricultural production, supply chains, and access to food, leading to shortages and increased prices.

**What role does climate change play in food security?**

Climate change impacts crop yields and agricultural productivity, making food systems more vulnerable to disruptions.

**What can individuals do to support food security?**

Individuals can support local agriculture, reduce food waste, and advocate for policies that promote sustainable food systems.

**How can governments mitigate the impact of conflicts on food security?**

Governments can invest in resilient agricultural practices, establish strategic food reserves, and promote international cooperation.

**What are the long-term implications of food insecurity?**
