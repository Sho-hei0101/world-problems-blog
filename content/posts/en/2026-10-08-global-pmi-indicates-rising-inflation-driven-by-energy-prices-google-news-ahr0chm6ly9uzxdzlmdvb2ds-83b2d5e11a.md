## FAQ

**What is the Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI)?**

The PMI is an economic indicator that gauges the health of the manufacturing and service sectors based on surveys of private sector companies.

**How does inflation affect my daily life?**

Inflation can lead to higher prices for goods and services, reducing your purchasing power and potentially impacting your savings and investments.

**What can businesses do to mitigate the effects of inflation?**

Businesses can streamline operations, adjust pricing strategies, and explore cost-saving measures to maintain profitability amidst rising costs.

**Why are energy prices a significant factor in inflation?**

Energy prices influence the cost of transportation and production, which can lead to broader price increases across various sectors.

**What role do central banks play in managing inflation?**

Central banks, like the Federal Reserve, adjust interest rates and implement monetary policy to control inflation and stabilize the economy.
