## FAQ

**What are the main causes of wildfires in Europe?**

Wildfires in Europe are primarily caused by a combination of climate change, human activities, and environmental conditions such as drought.

**How does climate change affect the frequency of wildfires?**

Climate change leads to higher temperatures and prolonged droughts, creating ideal conditions for wildfires to ignite and spread.

**What role do governments play in wildfire management?**

Governments are responsible for implementing policies, funding firefighting efforts, and coordinating responses to mitigate wildfire risks.

**Can individuals help reduce wildfire risks?**

Yes, individuals can help by maintaining their properties, following local fire regulations, and advocating for sustainable land management practices.

**What are the long-term implications of the wildfire blame game?**

The blame game can hinder effective collaboration and policy-making, making it more difficult to address the root causes of wildfires and climate change.
