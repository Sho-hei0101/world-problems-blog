## FAQ

**What factors contribute to rising mortgage rates?**

Rising inflation, central bank policies, and economic growth are key contributors.

**How do mortgage rates affect home buying?**

Higher rates increase monthly payments, reducing overall affordability for buyers.

**Should I buy a home now or wait for rates to drop?**

This depends on individual circumstances, market conditions, and financial readiness.

**What can I do to prepare for higher mortgage rates?**

Consider getting pre-approved for a mortgage and shop around for the best rates.

**Are there alternatives to traditional mortgages?**

Yes, options include adjustable-rate mortgages (ARMs) and government-backed loans.
