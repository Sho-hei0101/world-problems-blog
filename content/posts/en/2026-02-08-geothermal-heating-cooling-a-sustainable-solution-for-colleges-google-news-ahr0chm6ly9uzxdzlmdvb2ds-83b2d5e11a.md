## FAQ

**What is geothermal heating and cooling?**

Geothermal heating and cooling uses the Earth's natural temperature to regulate indoor climates, providing an energy-efficient alternative to traditional HVAC systems.

**How does a geothermal system save money?**

Geothermal systems reduce energy costs by utilizing stable ground temperatures, leading to lower utility bills and reduced reliance on fossil fuels.

**Is geothermal energy sustainable?**

Yes, geothermal energy is considered sustainable as it relies on the Earth's natural heat, producing minimal greenhouse gas emissions compared to conventional energy sources.

**What are the installation requirements for a geothermal system?**

Installation typically requires land for horizontal loops or deep boreholes for vertical systems, along with specialized equipment and skilled technicians.

**Can geothermal systems work in all climates?**

Geothermal systems can be effective in a variety of climates, but local geology and soil conditions can impact efficiency and installation feasibility.
