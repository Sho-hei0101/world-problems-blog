## Problem overview

Climate migration is increasingly becoming a pressing issue as people are forced to relocate due to extreme weather events, rising sea levels, and other environmental challenges. This movement not only puts pressure on resources in receiving areas but also has hidden energy costs associated with the relocation process. Home hardening, which involves reinforcing structures to withstand climate impacts, offers a potential solution to minimize the need for migration by making homes more resilient.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of climate migration are felt globally, affecting economies, social structures, and environmental policies. As more individuals and families are displaced, the strain on urban areas intensifies, leading to increased energy demand and resource competition. By investing in home hardening, communities can reduce the impacts of climate change on their residents, ultimately leading to a more sustainable future and less pressure on migration pathways.
