## Practical actions you can take

Being prepared for extreme weather is essential for safety and resilience. Here are some practical actions you can take to ensure you and your loved ones are ready for whatever the weather brings.

- Monitor local weather forecasts regularly.
- Create an emergency kit with essentials (water, food, first-aid supplies).
- Develop a family communication plan for emergencies.
- Identify safe locations for shelter during severe weather.
- Review your home insurance to ensure adequate coverage.

## FAQ

**What should I do during a tornado warning?**

Seek shelter in a sturdy building, preferably in a basement or an interior room away from windows.

**How can I prepare for a blizzard?**

Stock up on essential supplies, stay indoors, and have a plan for staying warm in case of power outages.

**What are the signs of an impending wildfire?**

Look for dry conditions, high winds, and smoke in the area. Have an evacuation plan ready.

**How can I stay informed about extreme weather?**

Follow local weather reports, download weather apps, and sign up for emergency alerts.

**What steps can I take to protect my home from flooding?**

Ensure proper drainage, elevate electrical appliances, and consider flood insurance.
