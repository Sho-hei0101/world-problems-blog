## FAQ

**How do geopolitical events affect mortgage rates?**

Geopolitical events can create uncertainty in the financial markets, impacting investor confidence and leading to fluctuations in interest rates.

**What role does the Federal Reserve play in mortgage rates?**

The Federal Reserve sets benchmark interest rates, which influence the rates lenders offer to borrowers. Changes in these rates can directly affect mortgage costs.

**Should I wait to buy a home until rates fall?**

While waiting for lower rates may seem appealing, it's essential to consider the overall housing market and your personal financial situation before making a decision.

**How can I stay informed about mortgage rate changes?**

Follow financial news sources, subscribe to mortgage rate alerts, and consult with financial advisors to keep updated on trends and forecasts.

**What should I do if rates rise before I buy?**

Consider locking in a rate with your lender, explore adjustable-rate mortgages, or reassess your budget and home search criteria.
