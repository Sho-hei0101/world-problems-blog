## Problem overview

The Bank of Canada has recently highlighted a concerning trend in the job market characterized by low hiring and firing rates, leading to what they describe as 'inertia.' This inertia can significantly impact economic growth and stability, creating challenges for job seekers and employers alike. With fewer new positions being created and a hesitance to let go of current employees, the labor market becomes less dynamic, stifling innovation and adaptability.

## Why this matters globally

This issue matters globally as job markets are interconnected. A stagnant job market can lead to reduced consumer spending, which in turn affects economic growth on a larger scale. Countries that rely heavily on exports may find their economies struggling if their trading partners are experiencing similar employment issues. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for policymakers and businesses seeking to foster a resilient economy.

## Practical actions you can take

To adapt to the current job market dynamics, both job seekers and employers can take several practical actions. These steps can help navigate the challenges presented by a low hire, low fire environment and promote a more dynamic job market.

- Network with professionals in your industry to explore opportunities.
- Stay informed about local job market trends and shifts.
- Consider upskilling or reskilling to enhance your employability.
- Evaluate your hiring practices if you're an employer to encourage innovation.

- Explore remote or flexible work options to broaden your job search.
- Monitor economic indicators to anticipate changes in the job market.
