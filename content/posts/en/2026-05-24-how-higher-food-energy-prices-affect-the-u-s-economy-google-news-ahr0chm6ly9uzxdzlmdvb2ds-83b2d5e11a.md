## FAQ

**What causes food and energy prices to rise?**

Food and energy prices can rise due to various factors, including supply chain disruptions, increased demand, climate change, and geopolitical tensions.

**How can consumers mitigate the impact of rising prices?**

Consumers can mitigate the impact by budgeting effectively, seeking discounts, buying in bulk, and exploring alternative energy sources.

**Is the U.S. economy likely to enter a recession due to rising prices?**

While rising prices can strain consumer spending, the U.S. economy has shown resilience and may not necessarily enter a recession.

**How do rising prices affect low-income households?**

Low-income households are often more severely affected by rising prices, as a larger portion of their income goes towards essentials like food and energy.

**What role do government policies play in addressing price increases?**

Government policies can help stabilize prices through subsidies, regulations, and support programs for low-income families.
