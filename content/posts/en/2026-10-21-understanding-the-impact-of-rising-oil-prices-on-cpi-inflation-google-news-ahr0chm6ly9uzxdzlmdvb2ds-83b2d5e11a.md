## FAQ

**What is CPI inflation?**

CPI inflation refers to the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index, which measures the average change over time in the prices paid by consumers for goods and services.

**How do oil prices affect inflation?**

Oil prices influence transportation and production costs, which can lead to higher prices for goods and services, thus contributing to overall inflation.

**What can consumers do to cope with rising inflation?**

Consumers can budget more effectively, seek alternatives for high-cost items, and consider energy-efficient practices to reduce overall expenses.

**Are there long-term impacts of rising oil prices?**

Yes, sustained high oil prices can lead to long-term economic adjustments, including shifts in consumer behavior, changes in energy policies, and potential recessions if inflation is not managed.

**How can governments respond to rising inflation?**

Governments may adjust interest rates, implement subsidies, or introduce fiscal measures to alleviate the impact on consumers and stimulate economic growth.
