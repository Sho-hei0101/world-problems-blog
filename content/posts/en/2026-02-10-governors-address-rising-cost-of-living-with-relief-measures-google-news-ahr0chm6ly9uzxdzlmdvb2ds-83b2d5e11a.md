## FAQ

**What are relief checks?**

Relief checks are direct payments made by the government to help individuals and families cope with financial burdens.

**How do tax cuts help with the cost of living?**

Tax cuts can increase disposable income for families, allowing them to spend more on necessities, thus boosting the economy.

**What housing policies are being considered?**
