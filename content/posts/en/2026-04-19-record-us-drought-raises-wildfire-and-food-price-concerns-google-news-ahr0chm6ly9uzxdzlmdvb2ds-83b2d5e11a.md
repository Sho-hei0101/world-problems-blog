## FAQ

**What causes droughts?**

Droughts are primarily caused by prolonged periods of below-average precipitation, exacerbated by factors like climate change, high temperatures, and increased evaporation rates.

**How do droughts affect food prices?**

Droughts can reduce crop yields significantly, leading to lower supply and higher prices for consumers. This can impact everything from staple foods to fresh produce.

**What can individuals do to conserve water during a drought?**

Individuals can reduce water usage by fixing leaks, using drought-resistant plants in landscaping, and being mindful of water consumption in daily activities.

**Are there any government programs to assist with drought relief?**

Yes, various federal and state programs provide assistance to farmers affected by drought, including financial aid and resources for water conservation.

**How can I stay informed about drought conditions?**

You can stay updated on drought conditions through government websites, local news outlets, and organizations dedicated to climate monitoring.
