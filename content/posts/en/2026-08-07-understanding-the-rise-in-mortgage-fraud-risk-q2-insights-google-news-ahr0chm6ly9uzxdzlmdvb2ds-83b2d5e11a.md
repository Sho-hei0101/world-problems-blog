## FAQ

**What is mortgage fraud?**

Mortgage fraud involves misrepresentation or deceit in obtaining a mortgage loan, often leading to financial loss for lenders.

**How can I identify potential mortgage fraud?**

Look for inconsistencies in financial documents, unusual loan terms, or pressure to act quickly without proper due diligence.

**What should I do if I suspect mortgage fraud?**

Report your suspicions to your lender and local authorities immediately to investigate the matter.

**Are there specific signs of increasing mortgage fraud risk?**

Signs include a sudden rise in loan applications with unverifiable income, inflated property values, and unusually high cash transactions.

**How can lenders protect themselves from mortgage fraud?**

Implementing robust verification processes, using fraud detection tools, and conducting regular audits can help mitigate risks.
