## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation can be caused by various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, rising production costs, and changes in monetary policy.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Energy prices directly impact transportation and production costs, which can lead to higher prices for goods and services across the economy.

**Are falling energy prices a sign of economic recovery?**

Not necessarily. While lower energy prices can provide relief, they may also signal reduced demand or other underlying economic challenges.

**What can consumers do to manage inflation?**

Consumers can budget wisely, seek alternatives for goods and services, and stay informed about market trends to make better financial decisions.

**How do governments respond to inflation?**

Governments may adjust interest rates, implement fiscal policies, or provide support to affected sectors to manage inflation.
