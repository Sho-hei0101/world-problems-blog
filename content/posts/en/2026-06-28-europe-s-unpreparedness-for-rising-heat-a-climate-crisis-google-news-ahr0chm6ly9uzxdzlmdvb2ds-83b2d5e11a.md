## Problem overview

Europe is facing an alarming rise in temperatures as climate change accelerates. Despite decades of scientific warnings and extensive research, the continent finds itself inadequately prepared to handle the escalating heat. Infrastructure, public health systems, and emergency responses are not aligned with the growing frequency and intensity of heatwaves, leading to dire consequences for both people and the environment.

## Why this matters globally

The implications of Europe's unpreparedness extend beyond its borders. As one of the world's largest economies, Europe's climate resilience influences global markets, international policy, and environmental standards. The health risks posed by extreme heat disproportionately affect vulnerable populations, including the elderly and those with pre-existing conditions, highlighting the urgent need for comprehensive climate action. Furthermore, the environmental impact of inaction can lead to biodiversity loss, food insecurity, and increased migration pressures worldwide.

## FAQ

**What are the main causes of rising temperatures in Europe?**

The primary causes include greenhouse gas emissions from human activities, deforestation, and industrial processes that contribute to global warming.

**How can individuals help combat climate change?**

Individuals can reduce their carbon footprint by using public transport, conserving energy, supporting renewable energy initiatives, and advocating for climate policies.

**What are the health risks associated with extreme heat?**

Extreme heat can lead to heat exhaustion, heatstroke, and exacerbate pre-existing health conditions, particularly in vulnerable populations.

**Are there specific policies being proposed to address this issue?**

Yes, many countries are proposing policies focused on renewable energy, urban planning adaptations, and public health initiatives to mitigate the effects of climate change.

**How can communities prepare for heatwaves?**

Communities can establish cooling centers, distribute information on heat safety, and improve green spaces to help mitigate urban heat effects.
