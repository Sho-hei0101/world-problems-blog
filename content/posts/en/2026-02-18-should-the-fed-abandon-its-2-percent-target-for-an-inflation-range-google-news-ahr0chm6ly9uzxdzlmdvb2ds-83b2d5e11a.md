## Problem overview

The Federal Reserve's long-standing target of maintaining a 2% inflation rate is increasingly being questioned. Economists and policymakers are debating whether this rigid target hinders the Fed's ability to respond to economic fluctuations effectively. With changing economic conditions, some suggest that adopting an inflation range could provide the Fed with more flexibility to navigate economic challenges. This post explores the arguments for and against this shift, providing a comprehensive overview of the potential impacts on the economy.
