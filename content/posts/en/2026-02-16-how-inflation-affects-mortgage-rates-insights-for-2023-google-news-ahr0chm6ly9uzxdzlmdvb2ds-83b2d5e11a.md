## FAQ

**How does inflation affect mortgage rates?**

Inflation typically leads to higher mortgage rates as lenders increase rates to compensate for the decreased purchasing power of money.

**What is core CPI?**

Core CPI measures the change in the price of goods and services, excluding food and energy, and is used to assess inflation trends.

**What should homebuyers do in a high-inflation environment?**

Homebuyers should consider locking in mortgage rates, exploring fixed-rate options, and staying informed about market trends.

**How can inflation expectations impact real estate investments?**

Expectations of rising inflation can lead to increased mortgage rates, affecting property values and rental income potential.

**What are some common strategies to mitigate the effects of inflation on mortgages?**

Consider refinancing options, opting for fixed-rate mortgages, and diversifying investments to hedge against inflation.
