## FAQ

**How much can I save by switching to solar energy?**

Savings vary by location, energy usage, and local incentives, but many homeowners report significant reductions in their energy bills.

**What are the initial costs of installing solar panels?**

Initial costs can be high, but many financing options and tax incentives are available to reduce the financial burden.

**Do I need a lot of sunlight for solar panels to work effectively?**

Solar panels can still generate energy on cloudy days, although more sunlight does enhance their efficiency.

**What maintenance do solar panels require?**

Solar panels require minimal maintenance, typically just occasional cleaning and inspections to ensure optimal performance.

**Are there government incentives for switching to solar?**

Yes, many regions offer tax credits, rebates, and incentives to encourage the adoption of solar energy.
