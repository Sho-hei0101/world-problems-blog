## FAQ

**How are property taxes calculated?**

Property taxes are typically calculated based on the assessed value of your property and the local tax rate.

**Can I appeal my property tax assessment?**

Yes, homeowners can often appeal their property tax assessments if they believe their property has been overvalued.

**What factors influence home insurance rates?**

Home insurance rates can be influenced by location, home value, age of the home, and the homeowner's claims history.

**Is it possible to lower my mortgage payments?**

Yes, refinancing, negotiating insurance rates, or appealing property taxes can help lower overall mortgage payments.

**What should I do if I can't afford my property taxes?**

If you can't afford your property taxes, consider reaching out to local agencies for assistance or exploring payment plans.
