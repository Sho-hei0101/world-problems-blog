## FAQ

**Why are mortgage rates rising?**

Mortgage rates are rising primarily due to inflation concerns and the Federal Reserve's policies aimed at controlling economic growth.

**How does inflation affect mortgage rates?**

When inflation rises, lenders often increase mortgage rates to compensate for the decreased purchasing power of money over time.

**What should home buyers do in a high-rate environment?**
