## FAQ

**How does geopolitics affect energy prices?**

Geopolitical tensions can lead to uncertainty in oil and gas supplies, causing prices to fluctuate.

**What is the relationship between energy prices and climate policy?**

Higher energy prices can accelerate the transition to renewable energy, but volatility can hinder investment in sustainable practices.

**Why should the U.S. consider global implications in its climate strategy?**
