The ongoing conflicts around the world have significant implications, particularly regarding energy costs. The UN General Assembly has highlighted the need for a green transition, emphasizing that military actions cannot control natural phenomena like wind, and thus, sustainable energy solutions are vital for future resilience.

## FAQ

**What are the main causes of rising energy costs due to war?**

Rising energy costs during conflicts are primarily due to supply chain disruptions, increased military demand for fuel, and geopolitical tensions that affect oil and gas markets.

**How can a green transition help mitigate these costs?**

A green transition can reduce dependence on fossil fuels, stabilize energy prices, and promote energy independence, which can lessen the impact of conflicts on energy supply.

**What role does the UN play in addressing energy costs related to war?**

The UN raises awareness of the interconnectedness of energy, climate, and conflict, encouraging member states to adopt sustainable energy practices and policies.

**How can individuals contribute to a green transition?**

Individuals can reduce energy consumption, support renewable energy initiatives, and advocate for policies that promote sustainability at local and national levels.
