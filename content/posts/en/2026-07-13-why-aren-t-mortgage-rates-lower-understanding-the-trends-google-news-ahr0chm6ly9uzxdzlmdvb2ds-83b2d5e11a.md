## Problem overview

Mortgage rates have remained stubbornly high despite expectations for a decline. Homebuyers and homeowners looking to refinance are left wondering why they are not benefiting from lower rates. Several economic factors contribute to this situation, including inflation, Federal Reserve policies, and global economic conditions. Understanding these dynamics is essential for making informed financial decisions in the housing market.

## Why this matters globally

The state of mortgage rates has a profound impact on the housing market, affecting affordability for buyers and the overall economy. High rates can deter potential buyers, leading to decreased demand and lower home prices. Conversely, lower rates typically stimulate the market, encouraging more transactions. This cycle affects not just individual homeowners but also the broader economy, as the housing market plays a critical role in economic stability and growth.
