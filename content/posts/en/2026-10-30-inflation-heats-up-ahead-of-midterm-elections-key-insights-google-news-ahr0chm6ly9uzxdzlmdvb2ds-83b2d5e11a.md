## FAQ

**What causes inflation to rise?**

Inflation can be caused by various factors including increased demand for goods and services, rising production costs, and supply chain disruptions.

**How does inflation affect my personal finances?**

Inflation decreases the purchasing power of money, meaning you can buy less with the same amount, impacting savings and expenditures.

**What can I do to protect my finances during inflation?**

Consider budgeting carefully, investing in assets that may appreciate, and seeking financial advice tailored to your situation.

**How is inflation measured?**

Inflation is typically measured using indices like the Consumer Price Index (CPI), which tracks the price changes of a basket of consumer goods.

**Will inflation affect my job security?**

While inflation may not directly impact job security, economic downturns influenced by high inflation can lead to layoffs and reduced hiring.
