## Why this matters globally

Rising energy prices have far-reaching implications for economies around the world. They can lead to inflation, affect consumer spending, and strain governmental budgets. Energy prices directly influence everything from transportation costs to food prices, making them a critical factor in global economic stability. Additionally, energy transitions toward sustainable sources are often hindered by fluctuating fossil fuel prices, complicating efforts to combat climate change.
