## Problem overview

Energy prices are subject to fluctuations influenced by a variety of factors including geopolitical tensions, supply and demand imbalances, natural disasters, and changes in government policies. Understanding these elements is crucial for consumers, businesses, and policymakers alike to navigate the complexities of the energy market effectively. The volatility in energy prices can lead to increased costs for consumers and businesses, affecting everything from household budgets to operational expenses for companies.
