## FAQ

**How do geopolitical events influence mortgage rates?**

Geopolitical events can affect inflation and economic stability, leading to changes in interest rates set by central banks.

**What role does the Federal Reserve play in mortgage rates?**

The Federal Reserve influences interest rates through its monetary policy decisions, impacting borrowing costs for mortgages.

**Should I wait to buy a home until mortgage rates fall?**

While lower rates are beneficial, waiting can be risky; it's essential to consider current market conditions and personal circumstances.

**How can I lock in a low mortgage rate?**

Consult with mortgage lenders about locking in rates when they are favorable, and consider getting pre-approved for a mortgage.

**What should I do if rates rise after I lock in?**

If you lock in a rate, you're protected from increases; however, if rates fall, you may want to discuss options with your lender.
