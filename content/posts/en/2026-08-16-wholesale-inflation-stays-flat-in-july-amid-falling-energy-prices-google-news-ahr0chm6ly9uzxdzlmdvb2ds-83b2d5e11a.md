## FAQ

**What is the Producer Price Index (PPI)?**

The PPI measures the average changes in prices received by domestic producers for their output, providing insights into inflation trends.

**How do energy prices affect inflation?**

Energy prices are a significant component of overall inflation, as they impact production costs and, consequently, consumer prices.

**Why is flat wholesale inflation significant?**

Flat wholesale inflation can indicate economic stagnation or stability, influencing business planning and consumer confidence.
