## Problem overview

The rise in global temperatures due to fossil fuel emissions has led to an alarming increase in heat-related deaths across Europe. As climate change intensifies, heatwaves have become more frequent and severe, posing significant health risks, particularly to vulnerable populations such as the elderly and those with pre-existing health conditions. The combination of rising temperatures and inadequate public health responses has resulted in thousands of preventable deaths, highlighting the urgent need for action to address this crisis.

## FAQ

**What are the main causes of heat-related deaths?**

Heat-related deaths primarily occur due to heat exhaustion, heatstroke, and exacerbation of pre-existing health conditions caused by extreme temperatures.

**How can communities prepare for heatwaves?**

Communities can implement public awareness campaigns, provide cooling centers, and promote green spaces to help mitigate the effects of heatwaves.

**What role do fossil fuels play in climate change?**

Fossil fuels release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere, leading to global warming and resulting in more frequent and severe heatwaves.

**How can individuals reduce their carbon footprint?**

Individuals can reduce their carbon footprint by using public transport, conserving energy at home, and supporting renewable energy initiatives.

**What are heat action plans?**

Heat action plans are strategies developed by local governments to address extreme heat events, focusing on public health, safety, and infrastructure resilience.
