## FAQ

**What are mortgage spreads?**

Mortgage spreads refer to the difference between the interest rates on mortgages and the yields on government bonds. A lower spread typically indicates more favorable borrowing conditions.

**How do mortgage spreads influence home sales?**

Improved mortgage spreads can lower borrowing costs for homebuyers, making homes more affordable and stimulating demand in the housing market.

**What should buyers consider in the current market?**
