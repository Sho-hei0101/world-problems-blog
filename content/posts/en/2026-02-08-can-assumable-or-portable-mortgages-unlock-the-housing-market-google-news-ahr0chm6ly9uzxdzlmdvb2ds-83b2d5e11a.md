## FAQ

**What is an assumable mortgage?**

An assumable mortgage allows a buyer to take over the seller's existing mortgage, potentially benefiting from lower interest rates.

**What is a portable mortgage?**

A portable mortgage enables homeowners to transfer their existing mortgage to a new property, maintaining their current interest rate and terms.

**Are assumable or portable mortgages available for all types of loans?**

Not all loans are assumable or portable; it depends on the lender's policies and the specific mortgage terms.

**How can I find out if a mortgage is assumable?**

Check with your lender or review the mortgage documents to see if there are clauses allowing for assumption.

**What should I consider before assuming a mortgage?**

Evaluate the terms of the existing mortgage, including interest rates, fees, and any potential penalties.
