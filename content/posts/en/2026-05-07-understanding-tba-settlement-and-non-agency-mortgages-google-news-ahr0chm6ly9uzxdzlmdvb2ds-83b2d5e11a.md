## FAQ

**What is a TBA settlement?**

A TBA settlement refers to a type of mortgage-backed security transaction where the specific loans are not identified at the time of the trade.

**What are non-agency mortgages?**

Non-agency mortgages are loans that are not backed by government-sponsored enterprises (GSEs) like Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, often catering to borrowers with unique financial profiles.

**How does AI improve due diligence in mortgages?**

AI tools enhance due diligence by automating data analysis, identifying risks, and streamlining the review process, leading to faster and more accurate assessments.

**Why is the age of a house important in mortgage decisions?**

The age of a house can affect its valuation, maintenance costs, and the type of financing available, influencing overall mortgage terms and borrower eligibility.

**What steps can I take to prepare for a mortgage application?**

Review your credit score, gather necessary documentation, assess your financial situation, and research mortgage options.
