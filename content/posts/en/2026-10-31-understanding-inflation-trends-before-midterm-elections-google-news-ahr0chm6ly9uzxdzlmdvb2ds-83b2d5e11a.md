## FAQ

**What causes inflation?**

Inflation can be caused by various factors, including increased demand for goods and services, supply chain disruptions, and changes in monetary policy.

**How does inflation affect my daily life?**

Inflation increases the prices of everyday goods and services, leading to a higher cost of living and reduced purchasing power.

**What can I do to cope with rising inflation?**

Consider budgeting more effectively, seeking cost-saving opportunities, and exploring alternative options for essential purchases.

**Are there any long-term effects of sustained inflation?**

Sustained inflation can lead to economic uncertainty, decreased consumer confidence, and potential changes in interest rates.
