## Practical actions you can take

Individuals and families can take several practical steps to navigate the current economic climate. Here are some actionable suggestions to manage food costs and enhance financial stability during this challenging period.

- Plan meals ahead to minimize food waste and costs.
- Shop at local markets or farmers' markets for fresh produce.
- Consider bulk buying non-perishable items when possible.
- Stay informed about government initiatives that may affect food prices.
- Explore community resources for food assistance if needed.
